> First, the mode is a choice styled game so you do have to pay attention to what you select. It seems it also runs off protomon levels of your protomon and boosts their score some. You will still do plenty fine for that, but just a heads up. 
> 
> The mode runs off insanity and when you reach your max, your run will end. So make sure to watch out for that. You gain insanity from losing to enemies and for each area you fail when in a biome. At a point it’s impossible to stop it as you get too far behind. Events or offers can reduce insanity like defeating some enemies, rest area and certain choices in the 2 choice  card paths.
> 
> Each time you explore a certain biome area, it will level up and become slightly harder. Though as you progress through runs, even low level ones can be extremely hard and get scaled up. So keep watch and try to level boosts if you can for all biomes. When an area has an exclamation mark, that means it’s above your protomon for that biomes score and most likely will gain some insanity. Avoid if u can but sometimes u can’t. This is good time to reroll adaptability if u get bad roll, by using the purple card i will talk about at bottom of guide.
> 
> Going through the mode, you will offered choices such as choosing which environment to explore, hatch more eggs, rest area, illusionist cards, a fountain, engage in combat, rarer events, fortuneteller and possibly more I forgot.

**Stone** (`stoney123123`) · 11:27:27 UTC
> - Hatch Eggs: Roll a slot to get between 1-3 eggs of a specific biome. These eggs are the protomon that boost your environment number to progress further in those biomes.
> 
> - Rest Area: Used to reduce insanity by a slot machine roll, but I was always hitting 28. May be more or less at times, but that was my average.
> 
> - Illusionist Cards: Stick to the free ones imo, but they offer cards to boost all environments score, bonuses for trio match on hatch and other things like that. You can also sometimes attack him for 2 free cards to keep watch if  you can win that. Can check and also back out of that option too.
> 
> - Fountain with Fairy: The spring with the fairy, if you soak in it you get the 30 insanity cap. If you do the other option you'll summon the fairy, can get 2 proto, 2 cards or something from the fairy,  nice 3 choice option.
> 
> - Engage in Combat: You fight a boss protomon that will allow you to recruit them after and also gain Astro cards and an environment boost usually. 
> 
> - Astro Cards: These will be used at the fortuneteller. Very big item to be farming for. These carry between events.
> 
> - Fortuneteller: The big rare find that offers unique items specific to Kaleido. Each spin requires 10 Astro cards. These rewards so far I have seen, are sss relic, exclusive dragon skin (frost drake I think), and a protomon form that gives 100 avatar score  instead of 30. These Astro cards will carry between weeks and the wheel does not reset each week. So spend when you see the fortuneteller, or can refresh it before spending if you want a different main reward. Just stick to that reward once you start spinning the wheel as you will just waste progress if you reset after doing spins. And once you get main reward, reset wheel to work on next one.

**Stone** (`stoney123123`) · 11:35:42 UTC
> \*\*\_\_Kaleido Tips:\_\_\*\*
> 
> - Your first run is free and cost no energy steele. 2nd+ is 1k energy steele.  So you are able to do more than 1 or as many as you can, as long as you have the energy. 
> 
> - Buy the ban relic for the Kaleido stars in shop asap to use in future runs. This allows you to ban lower score protomon to allow only stronger pulls in runs, but at the cost of using more  energy steele before entering  each run. 
> 
> All Blue Ban - 50 Energy \| 10 per ban
> All Purple Ban- 100 energy \| 10 per ban
> All S Ban - 600 energy  \| 10 per ban
> All SS Ban- 50 energy \| 10 per ban
> 
> So for full bans, you need 800 on first run and 1800 every run after. U could possibly get 500 off second run if you did terrible on first, but shouldn’t get using all those bans.
> 
> - When banning and short on energy, you can go back to specific protomon and unban some and it will cost less. As it cost energy per ban rather than always a static amount. Auto ban selects your worse score protomon each environment so don’t worry about that, but when removing bans, sort by environment and take the highest scored from each out of the bans until u get desired amount of energy you need to do a run.
> 
> -For an explanation of next part, 5/10/5/10 is referring to people getting their steele level to level 5 one week and level 10 the next week. This was used by free to play and light spenders who couldn’t get 10+ every week.  
> 
> - \*\*\_\_For 5/10/5/10 players\_\_\*\*, this is no longer the move with introduction of Kaleido. It is now changed to \*\*\_\_5/5/5/Highest you can get in Kaleido week.\_\_\*\* Kaleido is once a month. So starting next week, you will do 3 weeks of getting level 5 steele and during Kaleido (4th week), use all tokens to get 15 or even higher if u want.
> 
> For spenders who can get level 10 or even 15 every week, imo to best play Kaleido if there is no cap on number of runs, is to go for level 10 every week only and then on Kaleido week go crazy and spend all your tokens for a ton of runs.
