> \## General Exploration
> 
> 
> Exploration in the Rift is divided into several smaller regions (known as "Time-spaces" henceforth). At launch we should have access to the first 8 Time-spaces, each mirroring one of the 8 realms. Each Time-space is further divided into 5 areas with apostles (like a regular realm). 
> 
> With the launch of rift, we should have access to 40 new apostles and 176 minions (that's a lot of toner). Keep an eye out for Apostle number 2, dominating them will reward you with Lotto 2. I have included their stats below.

> 
> There is a key feature of the Rift that did not launch with the rest of the realm in other versions. This is usually referred to as "Rift Partners" within the community but may have a new name with the official release. 
> 
> Rift Partners are obtained by dominating various apostles in the realm and provide massive stat buffs (like 15% move speed). As we are unsure if this feature will be released with the rest of rift, I strongly recommend saving your speedups until it drops.

**PseudoTurtle** (`.pseudoturtle`) · 15:50:01 UTC
> \## INTEL
> 
> As I'm sure you have all heard, Rift has a lot of intel to work through. The first page alone has 283 upgrades totalling 175+ million Intel to max. Make sure you're using Liberty for the intel boost
> 
> Please see the attached image for the general upgrade order or a more detailed breakdown below
> 
> Upgrades 1 - 4 provide boosts to travel speed, cards, exploration tiles, and food capacity (actually needed in Rift)
> 
> 6 Increases damage and 7 Increases maps dropped (more loot)
> 
> 5 and 8-15 all provide buffs to exploration tiles and general exploration loot
> 
> 16-19 are expensive as hell and have 50 levels each. Each upgrade increases one of your HARD stats for defeating a new rift apostle
^msg-1215687984196624445
