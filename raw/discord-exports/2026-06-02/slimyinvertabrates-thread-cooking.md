> \# FAQ for Cooking
> \## How to unlock?
> Dominate Athena in Time Rift (9th realm, no, this is not a typo), then you will be visited by Clown Express in your mailbox. Pay him 1k wtads. Then you can find cooking area in your dormitory.
> \## What do I do here?
> Initial mission-based tutorial should teach you all the basics, but in general treat this system as a marathon, not a sprint. If you feel you can't progress further reliably, it is always fine to wait for another day.
> \## Yeah, but what should I \_\_really\_\_ do here?
> Buy recipes/skills (not ingredients, at least initially) from the shop, upgrade delivery, upgrade utensils, see if you can develop/upgrade new dishes, fullfill orders (of any quality) or sales targets (preferably orange quality), and try to hit that order/target. Once orders/target for a particular dish are completed you can drop it for the new shiny toy (maybe unless you've really got into excess of ingredients), and keep going.
> \## When it is optimal to stop trying to develop dishes?
> If you can hit Perfect heat, you should be able to progress it at about 15-20% visible chance. But that might change down the line.
> \## How do I unlock Mutant Partner?
> Have your Mutant at T8, unlock second delivery service, find octopus-looking dish, max it to orange and complete order for them.
> \## I am stuck, I can't develop any more dishes!
> Just wait for the next day and shop refresh. That is if you've unlocked partner skills.
> \## I am stuck, I don't have coins to buy X!
> If you urgently need to buy something (delivery upgrade or skill), you may consider cooking purple dish, but try to not overdo, because every dish you do not cook for order or at orange is lost opportunity.
> \## What is Mega Stew? When do I use it?
> It's just excess ingredient sink. If you are getting to limit in your storage, it is fine to cook some purple/blue ingredients in it. Their median price would be a bit above blue quality, but you are unlikely to use all blue and purples efficiently anyway.
