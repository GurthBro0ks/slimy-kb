> The formula of elemental damage is as following:
> 
> A = your snail attack
> 
> E = your elemental attack
> 
> Elemental damage = E x log5 ( 1 + A )
> 
> For example if your attack is 30000, elemental attack is 240, then your elemental damage is:
> 
> 240 x log5 (1 + 30000) = 1537
