> Guide to maximize glorium gain:
> 
> 1st step:
> 
> You will be  unlocking 2nd relic slots.  You will reset priest and devotee slots for 4 biomes, and putting it into one biome until it reaches 30k fervor.  Unlock relic slot and put relic in. In my opinion, put your best possible relics in each biomes relic spots, as long as it doesn’t give medals for a museum or anything like that. Now reset the priest and devotee of this biome and move to biome. Do this until you unlock all 2nd relics for every biome. Save 250 glorium before doing this (each biome costs 50) as I don’t know if u can reset without unlocking it first. But once you unlock it, u keep the slot open. Even if you don’t have the 30k on the biome after.  This is something you don’t need to worry about again, until it comes time you can get 60k fervor and u can repeat this process for all biomes. This will be some months away, as we are locked by caps on protomon until higher stars for priests/devotees. 
> 
> 
> 2nd Step: 
> 
> This is the step to min max your glorium gain and treat it like rift where you run farm setup 99% of time and swap to push every so often. First you will reset all biomes priest and devotees skills. Not the protomon itself. What we are doing here is taking a single biome, preferably one you have a glorium boost stamp on, and take it to 38k fervor. This is a HUGE boost to income and should nearly double that biomes daily gain, due to boost from reaching higher rating. And stop exactly at 38k if you can. Put rest of priest and devotee into next best glorium income biome. And keep putting the priest/devotee points in there until u get 38k and keep doing that. 
> 
> 3rd step: 
> 
> You will notice your glory stat is a lot lower, and this is due to the farm part of the process. I’d say every month or whatever u feel is best for you, u will push the apostles again. It is relatively cheap to be resetting as it’s 50 wtads per skill reset. Maybe 1-2k at most to do full process.
