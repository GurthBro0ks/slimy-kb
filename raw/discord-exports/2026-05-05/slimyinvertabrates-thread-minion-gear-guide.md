**SYT** (`syts6`) · 14:34:51 UTC
> Minion Gear:
> - There are 3 gear per minion type, except for zombies, which has 2.
> - Gear boosts minion stats. Compare minions unequipped for a fair comparison
> - Prioritize gear that boosts form % over non-form gear
> 
> example: 
> For zombie, upgrade legendary Dethrone Coffin and Mind Control Helmet
> For angel, upgrade Minerva Staff and the Sixth Path.
> 
> Apply leftover mats to gear that boosts secondary minion types or special stats (trace of Lilith, Mind Control Helmet, Sixth Path) if not using demon, don’t use Hulc MK, it’s bad.
> 
> Rocket Cabin:
> - Each rocket cabin boosts a minion type or all minions.
> - Relics and resonances enhance the effect of cabins that boost minion stats.
> 
> The third row of cabins boosts a specific minion type. Relics and resonances can boost hard stats in cabins
> 
> Focus on upgrading cabins that increase your minion type. example, if using a zombie, break Space-Sensing Radar, Rainbow Generator, and Gamma Ray down and Upgrade EMP as high as mats allow and use leftovers to rebuild the other 3.
> 
> Army Comp:
> - Optimal army for Species War and Minion Sim differ. Unless you’re cloning for the sake of cloning, clone your strongest minions.
> 
> For Species War:
> - Optimal minions:
>   - Demon and Mutant as they have high ATK and Rush
>   - A handful of high HP troop stats with low enough power in 1st slot can limit causalities 
> 
> For Minion Sim:
> 
> - Angels and Zombies are great choices.
> In Species War, you’ll survive all 20 rounds, while in Sim, you’ll die before round 20.
> -Angels and zombies high HP extend the fight to later rounds, allowing you to clear further.
> 
> Dragons are not optimal until t8.
> 
> Skins are crucial. If your minions are naked, replace them with another minion if possible.
> 
> For any minion type, prioritize orange skins over blue skins. The difference between orange and blue scales with your strength. See pics for reference.
