# Gear and Gear Soul Leveling Priority Guide

Prioritization order for leveling gear and gear souls, based on Taeyeon's guide with updates for current content. Originally rift-focused.

> **Note:** SMS has more value outside rift because damage % on snail is low without rift museum damage% boosts. SMS can be pushed earlier for other fights.

## Baseline Rift Priority (Pre-Rift 2/3 and Persia)

1. **100% Crit** — Only important if you want to remove RNG. You can retry fights until you land all crits, but it costs time.
2. **+5 Chucks + Big Bounce** — Activate set effect.
3. **+5 Excaladbolg**
4. **+8 Chucks + Big Bounce** — Including set effect. Either keep this soul at T1 or push to T4. If you can't push to T4, focus on next steps until you have enough resources.
5. **+5 SMS, +5 Drazgul** (P2W — F2P skips)
6. **+8 Excaladbolg** (P2W — F2P skips)
7. **+5 Key** for T1 effect
8. **+5 Trident** for crit damage
9. **+9 Chucks, +9 Excaladbolg**
10. Once ignore def is maxed, do whatever

## Farming Gear

- Ring should be your highest + at most times — **+9 Ring** is the farm priority.
- After ring, put as much as possible into **Tesla** and **Dragon Gear**.
- Explore red gear after that — diminishing returns beyond these.
- You can leave ring at +9 and still progress on push gear.

## F2P Notes

- Skip steps that require paid gear (Key, Draz, SMS).
- Scientist and Fang gear have minimal returns — some players don't even equip them. +5 of each is roughly 1-2 time chests and 5-6 extra dragon chest frags per rift run.
- **Curator's Helm** at orange provides good returns for wtads — worth leveling periodically if you're strapped for btads.
- If you decide to spend, the gear that enables SMS (virtual helmet) is recommended as the first purchase.

## Key Takeaways

- 100% crit is the universal first priority to eliminate RNG
- Chucks + Big Bounce set activation is always step 2
- SMS becomes high priority for any content beyond baseline rift
- Ring +9 is the farm priority; push gear is separate
- F2P players skip Key, Draz, and SMS — still viable for most content
- Curator's Helm at orange is a good source of wtads
- Fortress is situational — skip if already surviving 20 rounds
- Scientist and Fang have minimal returns; not worth prioritizing
