# Thread Kaleido Explanation And Tips

## Kaleido Valley Explained

Kaleido Valley is a choice-styled game mode where players navigate through various environments, making decisions that impact their run. The mode's difficulty scales with your Protomon levels, which provide a score boost.

### Core Mechanics

*   **Insanity**: This is the primary resource that dictates the length of your run. Reaching maximum Insanity will end your current run.
    *   **Gaining Insanity**: Losing to enemies, failing to clear areas within a biome.
    *   **Reducing Insanity**: Defeating certain enemies, visiting Rest Areas, and specific choices in 2-choice card paths.
*   **Biome Leveling**: Each time you explore a biome area, it levels up and becomes progressively harder. As you advance through multiple runs, even low-level biomes can become significantly scaled up in difficulty. It's advisable to level up boosts for all biomes if possible.
*   **Exclamation Marks**: An exclamation mark on an area indicates that its score requirement is above your current Protomon's score for that biome. Entering such an area will most likely result in gaining Insanity. Avoid these if possible, but if unavoidable, consider using a purple "reroll adaptability" card.

### Event Choices During a Run

During a Kaleido run, you will be presented with various choices:

*   **Explore Environment**: Choose which biome area to enter.
*   **Hatch Eggs**: A slot machine roll that grants 1-3 Protomon eggs for a specific biome. These Protomon boost your environment score, allowing you to progress further.
*   **Rest Area**: Reduces your Insanity. Observations suggest an average reduction of 28 Insanity per visit, though this can vary.
*   **Illusionist Cards**: Offers cards that can boost all environment scores, provide bonuses for trio matches on egg hatches, and other effects. Free cards are generally recommended. You may also have an option to attack the Illusionist for 2 free cards; you can check the combat outcome and back out if it's unfavorable.
*   **Fountain with Fairy**:
    *   **Soak in it**: Sets your Insanity cap to 30.
    *   **Summon the Fairy**: Presents a 3-choice option, potentially granting 2 Protomon, 2 cards, or other rewards.
*   **Engage in Combat**: Fight a boss Protomon. Winning allows you to recruit the boss, gain Astro Cards, and usually an environment boost.
*   **Rarer Events**: Other unique events may appear.
*   **Fortuneteller**: A rare and highly valuable encounter.

### Key Items & Rewards

*   **Astro Cards**: These are crucial items obtained from combat. They are used at the Fortuneteller and carry over between Kaleido events (weeks).
*   **Fortuneteller Rewards**: Each spin costs 10 Astro Cards. The wheel does not reset weekly.
    *   **Rewards**: SSS Relic, exclusive Dragon Skin (e.g., Frost Drake), and a Protomon form that grants 100 Avatar Score (compared to the usual 30).
    *   **Strategy**: Spend Astro Cards when you encounter the Fortuneteller. You can refresh the wheel *before* spending any cards to change the main reward. Once you start spinning, stick to the current main reward to avoid wasting progress. After obtaining the main reward, reset the wheel to work towards the next one.

### Kaleido Tips & Strategy

*   **Energy Steele Cost**: Your first run in Kaleido Valley is free. Subsequent runs cost 1,000 Energy Steele each.
*   **Ban Relic**: Purchase this relic with Kaleido Stars from the shop as soon as possible. It allows you to ban lower-score Protomon, ensuring stronger pulls during your runs.
    *   **Ban Costs (per run)**:
        *   All Blue Ban: 50 Energy Steele (10 per ban)
        *   All Purple Ban: 100 Energy Steele (10 per ban)
        *   All S Ban: 600 Energy Steele (10 per ban)
        *   All SS Ban: 50 Energy Steele (10 per ban)
    *   **Total Full Bans**: Approximately 800 Energy Steele for the first run, and 1,800 Energy Steele for subsequent runs (assuming you don't get a 500 discount from a terrible first run).
    *   **Managing Bans**: If you're short on Energy Steele, you can unban specific Protomon to reduce the cost, as the cost is calculated per ban. The auto-ban feature selects your worst-scoring Protomon for each environment. When manually removing bans, sort by environment and remove the highest-scored Protomon from the ban list until you reach your desired energy cost.
*   **Energy Steele Spending Strategy (for Protomon Gumball)**:
    *   **For Free-to-Play (F2P) and Light Spenders (Old Strategy)**: 5/10/5/10 (Steele levels per week).
    *   **New Recommended Strategy (Kaleido is monthly)**: 5/5/5/Highest you can get in Kaleido week. This means spending tokens to reach level 5 for three weeks, then using all available tokens during the 4th (Kaleido) week to achieve Steele level 15 or higher.
    *   **For Big Spenders (who can get level 10-15 weekly)**: Aim for level 10 every week, then "go crazy" and spend all tokens on Kaleido week for maximum runs. If you barely reach level 15 after 4 weeks, consider a 5/10/5/All tokens on Kaleido approach. The decision often involves weighing Gumball rewards from weekly level 10 against extra Kaleido rewards.
*   **Multispeed Relic**: Costs 6 Kaleido Stars. Allows runs at 1x, 2x, or 3x speed. This is a quality-of-life improvement for faster runs, but not essential. It's generally recommended to save Kaleido Stars for unique items like the Protomon form (10 stars) rather than speed.
*   **Reroll Environment Boost Card**: This card can be found by clicking the Protomon symbol with your score below it in an area. It's a purple card with a face. Use it if you find yourself severely lacking adaptability or have a very low score at the start of an area, hoping for a better roll.
*   **Token Spending**: On your final run, if you're low on energy and cannot afford another full run, use any remaining energy on Protomon egg refreshes to avoid wasting it. Min-max your token spending each Kaleido event.
*   **Energy from Tokens**: Be aware that there's approximately a 42% chance to gain any energy from a token. You'll need at least 1,000 Energy Steele to start a run, and ideally 1,800 for full Protomon bans.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Insanity Management is Key**: Monitor your Insanity levels, as reaching the maximum ends your run. Prioritize reducing it through Rest Areas or specific choices.
*   **Astro Cards & Fortuneteller are High Priority**: Farm Astro Cards to spin the Fortuneteller wheel for valuable rewards like SSS relics and unique forms. Plan your spins carefully.
*   **Strategic Energy Steele Spending**: Adapt your Protomon Gumball spending strategy (e.g., 5/5/5/All on Kaleido week) to maximize runs and rewards during the monthly Kaleido event.
*   **Utilize Ban Relic**: Invest in the Ban Relic early to improve Protomon pulls and optimize your run efficiency, understanding the associated Energy Steele costs.
*   **Consult External Guides**: Leverage the provided PDF guides, especially the "Astrology Style" one, to make informed decisions during your runs and optimize outcomes.
