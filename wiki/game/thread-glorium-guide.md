# Thread Glorium Guide

This guide outlines a strategy to optimize Glorium gain in Super Snail, focusing on efficient management of Protomon Priests and Devotees. The method involves alternating between a farming setup for daily Glorium and a combat setup for pushing Apostles.

## Unlocking Second Relic Slots (Initial Setup)

The first step is to unlock the second relic slot for all biomes. This is a one-time process for each slot level (30k Fervor, then later 60k Fervor).

1.  **Reset Fervor:** Reset the Priest and Devotee skill points for four biomes.
2.  **Focus One Biome:** Allocate all available Priest and Devotee skill points into a single biome until it reaches 30,000 Fervor.
3.  **Unlock Slot:** Unlock the second relic slot for that biome. Each slot costs 50 Glorium, so save at least 250 Glorium before starting this process.
4.  **Equip Relic:** Place your best possible relic into the newly unlocked slot. Prioritize relics that boost stats rather than those primarily for museum medals.
5.  **Repeat:** Reset the Priest and Devotee skills for that biome, then move to the next biome and repeat the process until all biomes have their second relic slot unlocked.

**Important Notes:**
*   Once a relic slot is unlocked, it remains open even if the biome's Fervor drops below 30,000.
*   This process will need to be repeated for the 60,000 Fervor relic slot when Protomon caps allow for higher Priest/Devotee star levels.
*   Resetting Priest/Devotee skills costs 50 Wtads per skill.

## Maximizing Daily Glorium (Farming Setup)

After unlocking all second relic slots, the goal shifts to maximizing daily Glorium income. This setup is designed for consistent farming.

1.  **Full Fervor Reset:** Reset *all* Priest and Devotee skill points across all biomes.
2.  **Target 38k Fervor:** Focus all skill points into a single biome, preferably one with a Glorium boost stamp, until it reaches exactly 38,000 Fervor. Reaching this threshold provides a significant boost to that biome's daily Glorium gain, potentially nearly doubling it.
3.  **Distribute Remaining Points:** Allocate any remaining Priest and Devotee skill points to the next best Glorium income biome, aiming for 38,000 Fervor there, and continue this process for subsequent biomes.
4.  **Lower Glory:** Be aware that this concentrated Fervor setup will result in a lower overall "Glory" stat, which impacts Apostle combat effectiveness.

## Pushing Apostles (Combat Setup)

Periodically, you will need to switch to a combat-focused setup to push Apostles and unlock more Devotee slots. This is typically done every month or two, or as needed.

1.  **Full Fervor Reset:** Reset all Priest and Devotee skill points across all biomes.
2.  **Balance Fervor:** Distribute Priest and Devotee skill points as equally as possible across all biomes to maximize your overall "Glory" stat, which is crucial for Apostle fights.
3.  **Push Apostles:** Engage in Apostle combat to progress and unlock new Devotee slots.
4.  **Revert to Farming:** Once you can no longer push Apostles effectively, reset your Fervor again and revert to the 38,000 Fervor farming setup to continue maximizing Glorium income.

**Cost:** Resetting skills for this process is relatively cheap, costing around 50 Wtads per skill reset, totaling perhaps 1,000-2,000 Wtads for a full reset and redistribution.

## Terminology and Resetting Fervor

*   **Priest:** The main Protomon unit for each biome (circled in red in the original Discord image).
*   **Devotees:** The other Protomon units associated with each biome.
*   **Fervor:** The combined power level of a biome's Priest and Devotees, which determines Glorium income and relic slot unlocks.
*   **Resetting Fervor:** To reset Priest/Devotee skill points:
    1.  Click on the Priest in the Protomon interface.
    2.  Click the tabs in the top right corner of the Priest's detail screen.
    3.  Select the "Reset" option at the bottom.

## Benefits and Considerations

*   **Significant Glorium Boost:** Players have reported roughly a 20% increase in Glorium per biome, with some achieving 270+ Glorium daily after implementing this strategy.
*   **Early Game Viability:** This method is beneficial even for players without Lotto 3 or many Protomon relics, as the Fervor thresholds provide a substantial boost regardless of relic stats.
*   **Strategic Resource Allocation:** This guide emphasizes treating Glorium generation like Rift farming, where you optimize for income most of the time and switch to a push setup when necessary.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Prioritize 30k Fervor for Relic Slots:** Initially, focus on reaching 30k Fervor in each biome sequentially to unlock all second relic slots, then reset and move to the next.
*   **Target 38k Fervor for Farming:** For daily Glorium income, concentrate Priest/Devotee points into biomes to reach exactly 38k Fervor, starting with the highest income biome.
*   **Balance Fervor for Apostle Pushes:** When pushing Apostles, reset and distribute Fervor evenly across all biomes to maximize your "Glory" stat.
*   **Cost-Effective Switching:** Resetting Priest/Devotee skills is relatively inexpensive (50 Wtads per skill), making it feasible to switch between farming and pushing setups.
*   **Early Game Impact:** This strategy provides a noticeable Glorium boost even for players with limited Protomon relics or progression.
