# Thread Minion Talents

## Minion Talents Overview

To reach max level for Minion Talents, relics must be upgraded from SSS to Awakened.

**Relic Availability:**
*   All Lotto 1 Relics are generally available in Relic Fragment (RF) exchanges, with the notable exception of Quantum Radio.
*   Some Lotto 3 Relics become RFable after unlocking Godfall.
*   Other Lotto 2 and Lotto 3 Relics may or may not be RFable as specified below.

**Organ Availability:**
*   Organs listed in **bold** are currently buyable via Tickets or are found in the Mutantigen Pool.
*   Some Dragon organs are noted as potentially being "pay organs."

## Key Takeaways

*   Maxing Minion Talents requires relics to be Awakened (SSS quality).
*   Relics are primarily obtained through Lotto events, with many Lotto 1 relics available via Relic Fragment (RF) exchange, but Quantum Radio is a notable exception.
*   Specific organs are also required for certain minions, obtainable through Tickets, the Mutantigen Pool, or potentially as premium "pay organs."
*   The availability of Lotto 3 relics in RF exchange often depends on unlocking Godfall.
*   The required relics and organs vary significantly for each of the 30 unique minions across the six minion types (Zombie, Demon, Angel, Mutant, Mecha, Dragon).
