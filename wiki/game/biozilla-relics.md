# Biozilla Relic Guide

Optimal relic setups for each Biozilla fight. Both P2W (mine) and F2P versions provided where applicable. Many recurring relics appear across fights — get these to at least 5 star.

## General Notes

- Many good relics can be refined (RF) for all/specific wars — very helpful.
- **Enuma Elish** and similar relics are top tier across multiple fights.
- Prioritize relics that maintain high **civ** and **faith** affect numbers — don't sacrifice too much attack for utility.
- **R'lyeh** is a great fill-in relic for both P2W and F2P setups.

## Dung Beetle

- Use **Celestial Mechanics** in the fame slot if you have it at 5 star.
- Fill remaining slots with high civ/faith relics.

## Goldfish Biozilla

### P2W Version
- Optimized with premium relics for maximum affect.

### F2P Version
- Substitute premium relics with accessible alternatives maintaining civ/faith balance.

## Clam Biozilla

- If you have **5 star Crime and Punishment**, use it in the **civ slot**.
- Use **Harem** or **Black Body** in the **art slot**.
- F2P alternatives available for each slot.

## Hamster Biozilla

- Use **UN Forest** in the fame slot if you have it at 5 star.
- Fill remaining slots with best available civ/faith relics.

## Mantis Biozilla

- This fight is **very P2W** for good relic options.
- **Pillar of Creation** in the **Fame slot** if you have it at 5 star — this is a priority.
- Fill missing slots with what you can from the recommended list.
- **Tech and art slots** don't affect the affect number as much, so fill with what you have.
- A good purple relic with **4.5% ATK** can substitute if you need something and don't have **Qin Bronze** (at least purple stamps).

### F2P Mantis Fill-ins
- **R'lyeh** is strongly recommended on either P2W or F2P setup.
- Other fill-in relics maintain civ and faith while being accessible.

## Relic Comparison: Ivan the Terrible vs Harem Wall Painting

- **Ivan the Terrible**: 9% rush/def, but **low civ** — you lose a ton of attack.
- **Harem Wall Painting**: Better civ and attack stats.
- Swapping from a 6% rush relic to Ivan's 9% rush costs **3.8% ATK** — net loss.
- **Exception**: Ivan is very good for **push purge content** due to the 9% rush. Use it there or in the tech slot if missing something, but not in civ.

## Key Takeaways

- Get recurring Biozilla relics to at least 5 star — they appear across multiple fights
- Pillar of Creation (5 star) is mandatory for Mantis Biozilla fame slot
- Ivan the Terrible is bad for civ slot (low civ, loses ATK) but great for purge push content
- R'lyeh is a great universal fill-in for both P2W and F2P setups
- Maintaining high civ/faith affect is more important than individual stat boosts
- Enuma Elish is top tier across multiple Biozilla fights
- Tech and art slots are more flexible — fill with what you have
