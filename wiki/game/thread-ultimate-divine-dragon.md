# Thread Ultimate Divine Dragon

## Ultimate Divine Dragon and Dragon Orbs (Dorbs)

The Ultimate Divine Dragon event revolves around the collection and submission of "Dragon Orbs," often referred to as "dorbs" by players. This system tracks the total number of Dragon Orbs you have ever submitted throughout your gameplay, unlocking cumulative rewards.

### How Dragon Orbs are Counted

The Divine Dragon's progress is based on the **cumulative total** of all Dragon Orbs you have ever submitted. This includes orbs offered for any purpose, such as those used in other game mechanics or events. The game keeps a running tally, meaning you don't lose progress even if you've spent orbs elsewhere.

### Triggering the Event

To trigger the Divine Dragon event and check your progress or claim rewards, you simply need to offer a Dragon Orb.

### Rewards

A comprehensive list of rewards for various Dragon Orb submission milestones was provided via images (IMG_4479.png through IMG_4494.png). While the full list is extensive, specific milestones mentioned include:

*   **230 Dragon Orbs:** Unlocks a "nice relic."
*   **1150 Dragon Orbs:** A significant milestone, indicating high-tier rewards are available at this level.

### Player Tips

*   **Start collecting early:** Since the count is cumulative, every Dragon Orb you submit contributes to your progress towards the Divine Dragon's rewards.
*   **"Gobble down some dorbs":** Actively seek out and submit Dragon Orbs to advance your progress.

## Key Takeaways

*   The Ultimate Divine Dragon rewards are based on the **cumulative total** of all Dragon Orbs (dorbs) ever submitted.
*   Offering any Dragon Orb will trigger the event and allow you to view your progress and claim rewards.
*   Specific milestones mentioned include a "nice relic" at 230 dorbs and significant rewards at 1150 dorbs.
*   All Dragon Orbs submitted, regardless of their initial use in other game mechanics, count towards the Divine Dragon's total.
