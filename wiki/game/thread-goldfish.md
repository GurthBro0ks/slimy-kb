# Thread Goldfish

## Goldfish Event Guide

The Goldfish event involves players using their minions for both defense and offense in a structured daily schedule.

### Chronicle

The Chronicle is where you manage your minions for the event.

#### Defense
*   **Minion Selection**: Choose minions with high Defense, HP, HP regeneration, "take injury for other minions" abilities, and recovery speed/self-recovery.
*   **Daily Assignment**: You must assign your defensive minions each day before a specific daily reset time (local time). Failure to do so will leave your castle undefended.

#### Offense
*   **Minion Selection**: Choose minions with high Attack, Rush, and Elemental damage (Earth, Wind, Fire, Water, Poison).
*   **Daily Attacks**: Each day, you can make two attacks with your offensive minions.
*   **Target Selection**: Prioritize targets that will yield the most "flying carp."
*   **Diminishing Returns**: Avoid attacking the same target twice in a day, as it will result in fewer carp rewards for the second attack.
*   **Schedule**:
    *   **Saturday - Monday**: Attacks are against PVE (Player vs. Environment) enemies.
    *   **Tuesday - Thursday**: Attacks are against other PVP (Player vs. Player) clubs.
*   **Defense Setup for Next Day**: After a specific daily reset time, you can set up your defense for the following day.

### Mizutengu

*   **Underwater Agarwood**: Deposit your Underwater Agarwood into the Mizutengu.
*   **Persistence**: This resource carries over from week to week, making it a long-term goal. There is no need to save them up for specific periods.

### Battle Prediction

The game provides a clear indication of the battle outcome before you commit to a fight:
*   A **red bar** with the word "Defeat" indicates you will lose the fight.
*   A **blue bar** with the word "Win" indicates you will win the fight.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Daily Defense is Crucial**: Assign your defensive minions daily before the reset to avoid being undefended.
*   **Strategic Offense**: Make two attacks daily, prioritizing targets for "flying carp" and avoiding repeat attacks on the same target for maximum rewards.
*   **Minion Specialization**: Tailor your minion teams for Defense (HP, Defense, Recovery) and Offense (Attack, Rush, Elemental Damage).
*   **Mizutengu is a Long-Term Goal**: Consistently deposit Underwater Agarwood as it accumulates over time.
*   **Utilize Battle Prediction**: Always check the win/loss indicator to ensure successful attacks.
