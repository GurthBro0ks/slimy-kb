# Thread Gurth Needs A Gear Update

## Gear Progression for Rift and Farming

This guide outlines gear, organ, and relic strategies for progressing in the Rift and optimizing for farming, based on expert player advice.

### General Gear Strategy

*   **Prioritize Rift Progression:** Focus on a dedicated "push" gear setup for Rift, then switch back to a "farm" setup.
*   **Zombie T8 vs. Dragon Weapon:** If you have the T7 Dragon weapon with Ignore Defense, Zombie gear becomes less critical.
*   **Breaking Down Gear:** Consider breaking down Serket Staff to reallocate resources.
*   **Gear Soul:** Activate the Valkyrie gear soul (one past the gear shrine) once you have +5 Chucks and Big Bounce.

### Specific Gear Recommendations

**Rift Push Setup:**
This setup is designed for maximum combat effectiveness in Rift and other challenging content.

*   **Weapons:**
    *   Chucks: +5
    *   Big Bounce: +5
    *   Excalabolg: +5 (requires 30 Heaven Wings to craft/upgrade)
    *   SMS (Super Snail Sword): +4 (aim for +3 initially, requires 30 Abyss Wings for +3)
    *   Draz (Dragon Claw): +3
    *   Trident: Allocate remaining upgrade levels here.
*   **Head:** Dracula Cap.
*   **Avoid/Downgrade:**
    *   Parsia gear
    *   Hammer
    *   Key
    *   Fortress (downgrade from current levels if needed for other gear)
    *   Zombie Stick (if Dragon weapon with Ignore Defense is available)
*   **Hulc:** Generally not recommended for push. It's only effective in goldfish fights against other players with elemental damage, or if your entire army is composed of demons. For general combat and gene simulations, there are better options.

**Farm Setup:**
This setup focuses on resource generation and general exploration.

*   **Ring:** Upgrade as high as possible (+8 or +9).
*   **Other Gear:** Allocate remaining levels to other attack-oriented gear as desired.
*   **Hulc vs. Mutant Boots:** For gene simulations, Mutant Boots are preferred over Hulc. If you can only have one red gear, use Mutant Boots. Keep Hulc for species fights if applicable.

**Minion Gear (for Gene Sim):**

*   **Dragon Gear:** +4
*   **Mind Control Helmet:** +2
*   **Angel Shield:** Allocate leftover levels.
*   **Mutant Boots:** Allocate leftover levels.
*   **Hulc:** Swap out Hulc for Mutant Boots in this setup.

### Organ Setup

Organs are crucial and have different optimal configurations for various game modes. Note that upgrading organs is a long-term process, and there are no save slots for organ setups outside of their respective game modes.

*   **General Recommendation:** Use Golden Snail and Golden Bird.
*   **Dominating:** Keep your current setup, but remove the "straight left organ" (leave the slot empty).
*   **Rift Push:** Swap Cuneiform and Scarab for Golden Snail and Golden Bird.
*   **Arena & Gene Simulation:**
    *   For the "Ab" and "top right eye organ" slots, use red organs (if you don't have specific ones).
    *   **Cosmos:** Use Cosmos for both of these slots. It can be effective even at T0, but prioritize upgrading it after reaching T40 Maat.

### Relic (Compass) Setup

Relics in the compass also have distinct setups for push and farm.

*   **Rift Push:**
    *   Keep Unispark.
    *   Place Golden Bird in the Cuneiform slot.
*   **Farm:**
    *   **Winged Victory:** Use this (assuming it's maxed).
    *   **Voyager:** Use Voyager if it is 5-star. If not, use Golden Bird instead.
    *   **Holy Loaves:** When switching from push to farm, replace Unispark with Holy Loaves.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Specialized Setups:** Maintain distinct gear, organ, and relic setups for "Rift Push" and "Farming" to maximize efficiency in each activity.
*   **Prioritize Key Upgrades:** Focus on bringing Chucks, Big Bounce, Excalabolg, SMS, and Draz to recommended levels for Rift pushes, and the Ring for farming.
*   **Organ Optimization:** Don't overlook organs; Golden Snail and Golden Bird are generally strong, with Cosmos being important for Arena/Gene Sim.
*   **Hulc's Limited Utility:** Hulc gear is situational and generally not recommended for most combat scenarios outside of specific elemental or demon-heavy builds.
*   **Crafting Requirements:** Be aware of crafting needs for key gear like Excalabolg (30 Heaven Wings) and SMS (30 Abyss Wings).
