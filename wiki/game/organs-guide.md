# Organs Setup Guide

Organ configurations for Arena, Pushing, Dominating, and Gene Sim. Different game modes require different organ setups.

## Getting Started: Seasonal Shop Priority

1. Go into the **Seasonal Shop** with gold punch points from arena.
2. Buy the **3 missing red organs** (1 copy each so you can get copies in mutigens):
   - **Gold Finger** (priority)
   - **Mecha Arm** (priority)
   - **Invisible Wing** (priority)
3. Once unlocked, buy the **2,500 packs** with gold points only — this is the only way to get those organs.
4. Choose a middle organ to take to **T2**, then swap to the eye organ until T2.

## Arena Setup

- **Orange helmet (top left)** vs **red helmet**: The orange provides slightly less ATK% but adds crit damage %, which performs better overall.
- The other 2 orange organs are **essential**:
  - If running **Cosmos**, you get **2 revives** per fight.
  - Using **Mirror** still gives **1 revive**.
  - The left orange organ provides a solid heal.
- **Bottom "texture" organ**: Swap out the dodge organ. Arena dodge % is generally worthless because most players have 100%+ hit, causing 0% dodge chance.

### Arena Organ Tips

- Experiment between the orange and red helmets to see what works best for your stats.
- The revive organs (cosmos/mirror setup) are game-changing for arena survival.
- If you don't have the abdomen orange organ from apostle purges, use the red one instead.

## Pushing Areas Setup

- Experiment between orange and red organs.
- Orange tends to be better than red for pushing, but trial and error is key.
- Gene sim and arena share a similar base setup — if you don't have the abdomen orange from apostle purges, use the red one.

## Dominating Setup

- **Remove one organ intentionally** for dominations — certain organs can cost you a domination due to RNG.
- The goal is to guarantee consistent damage output rather than relying on random procs.
- With the organ removed, you're guaranteed **530% of your ATK as damage in round 3**:
  - 210% from pre-round
  - 210% from attacking organs (whichever procs)
  - 110% from on-hit texture organ
- If an organ RNG procs in round 3 when you need 4, it can ruin the domination — removing it eliminates that risk.

## Gene Sim Setup

- Dedicated gene sim organ configuration optimized for extended fights.
- Prioritize HP and sustain organs to survive more rounds.

## Awakening Priority

- Message experienced players for specific awakening advice — it's highly case-dependent on what organs and content you're running.
- Generally focus awakening on organs used across multiple setups.

## Key Takeaways

- Buy Gold Finger, Mecha Arm, and Invisible Wing first from seasonal shop
- Arena: orange helmet (crit dmg) > red helmet; use revive organs (cosmos/mirror)
- Arena dodge organ is worthless — swap it out for something useful
- Dominations: remove risky RNG organs to guarantee consistent round 3 damage output
- Push vs Arena vs Gene Sim all require different organ setups — maintain separate configurations
- If missing the abdomen orange organ from apostle purges, the red equivalent works
- Awakening priority depends on your specific organ roster — ask experienced players
