# Thread Gear Leveling Guide

## Gear Leveling Priorities

This guide outlines gear and gear soul leveling priorities, primarily focusing on pushing content like Rifts, Protomon Chiefs, and Persia. Farming gear priorities differ and are discussed separately.

### General Gear Leveling Priorities (Rift Focused)

This foundational list provides a general progression for gear leveling. Note that SMS (Super Mecha Snail), Drazgul, and Key are typically considered paid gear.

1.  **100% Crit:** Prioritize achieving 100% critical hit chance to remove combat RNG. While theoretically possible to reset fights until critical hits land, consistent crit significantly saves time.
2.  **+5 Chucks + Big Bounce:** Level Chucks to +5 to activate its set effect (Big Bounce).
3.  **+5 Excaladbolg:** Level Excaladbolg to +5.
4.  **+8 Chucks + Big Bounce:** Continue leveling Chucks to +8, including its set effect. (Consider keeping its soul at T1 or pushing all the way to T4; if T4 is not feasible, focus on subsequent steps until resources allow).
5.  **+5 SMS, +5 Drazgul:** Level Super Mecha Snail (SMS) and Drazgul to +5.
6.  **+8 Excaladbolg:** Level Excaladbolg to +8.
7.  **+5 Key:** Level Key to +5 for its T1 effect.
8.  **+5 Trident:** Level Trident to +5 for its critical damage bonus.
9.  **+9 Chucks, +9 Excaladbolg:** Continue leveling Chucks and Excaladbolg to +9.
10. **Ignore Def:** Once ignore defense is maxed, further leveling priorities become more flexible.

**F2P Consideration:** Free-to-play players should generally skip paid gear like SMS, Drazgul, and Key, focusing on the other available gear.

### Farming Gear Priorities

Farming gear focuses on maximizing resource generation rather than combat effectiveness.

*   **Ring:** The Ring should generally be your highest leveled gear for farming. Aim for at least +9.
*   **Tesla & Dragon Gear:** Invest resources into these as much as possible for farming benefits.
*   **Explore Red Gear:** Prioritize leveling red gear that provides exploration bonuses.
*   **Scientist & Fang:** Some players find the return on Scientist and Fang to be minimal (e.g., +5 might yield only 1-2 extra time chests and 5-6 extra dragon chest fragments per Rift run). They may choose to save W-tads by not equipping or leveling these for farming.
*   **Curator's Helm:** Maxing its returns at Orange rarity is a good stopping point for this gear, as it provides W-tads.

### Specific Gear Notes

*   **Ring:** Primarily for farming. While it should often be your highest + for resource generation, for dedicated pushing, other combat-focused gear takes precedence.
*   **Key & Drazgul:** These are typically paid gear. F2P players usually skip them. If you decide to spend, acquiring the Virtual Helmet (which enables SMS) is a strong recommendation.
*   **Fortress:** For Home Fights, if your snail can survive 20 rounds without it, you can skip investing heavily in Fortress and reallocate resources.
*   **Scientist & Fang:** The benefits from these for farming (e.g., time chests, dragon chest fragments) are often considered low compared to the W-tads required for gear swaps and leveling.
*   **Curator's Helm:** Leveling this to Orange rarity is generally sufficient to maximize its W-tad generation.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Prioritize 100% Crit:** Achieving maximum critical hit chance is crucial for consistent combat performance and reducing RNG.
*   **Chucks & Excaladbolg are Core:** These two pieces of gear are fundamental offensive components and should be leveled consistently.
*   **Paid Gear (SMS, Drazgul, Key) Offer Significant Pushing Power:** While F2P players can skip them, these items provide substantial advantages for high-end content pushes.
*   **Farming vs. Pushing:** Gear leveling priorities differ significantly between maximizing resource generation (Ring, Tesla, Dragon Gear) and optimizing for combat challenges (Chucks, Excaladbolg, SMS).
*   **Content-Specific Strategies:** Tailor your gear leveling path to the specific content you are tackling, whether it's Rift, Persia, or Home Fights.
