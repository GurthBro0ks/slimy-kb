# Minion Gear and Army Composition Guide

Guide for minion gear selection, rocket cabin upgrades, and army composition for Species War and Minion Sim.

## Minion Gear Basics

- Each minion type has **3 gear pieces**, except zombies which have **2**.
- Gear boosts minion stats — compare minions **unequipped** for a fair comparison.
- Prioritize gear that boosts **form %** over non-form gear.

### Recommended Gear Priority

- **Zombie:** Upgrade legendary **Dethrone Coffin** and **Mind Control Helmet**.
- **Angel:** Upgrade **Minerva Staff** and **Sixth Path**.
- Apply leftover materials to gear that boosts secondary minion types or special stats (Trace of Lilith, Mind Control Helmet, Sixth Path).
- **Do NOT use Hulc MK unless using Demon** — it's bad otherwise.

## Rocket Cabins

- Each rocket cabin boosts a minion type or all minions.
- **Third row of cabins** boosts a specific minion type — relics and resonances can enhance these.
- **Focus on upgrading cabins that increase your primary minion type.**

### Example (Zombie main):
- Break down **Space-Sensing Radar**, **Rainbow Generator**, and **Gamma Ray**.
- Upgrade **EMP** as high as materials allow.
- Use leftovers to rebuild the other 3.

## Army Composition

Optimal army differs between **Species War** and **Minion Sim**. Clone your strongest minions unless cloning for the sake of cloning.

### Species War

- **Demon and Mutant** are optimal — high ATK and Rush.
- Place a handful of high HP troop stat minions with low enough power in the **first slot** to limit casualties.
- First slot is determined by lowest overall power (auto-sorted).

### Minion Sim

- **Angels and Zombies** are great choices — high HP extends fights to later rounds.
- In Species War you survive all 20 rounds; in Sim you'll die before round 20, but the extended fights let you clear further.
- **Dragons** are not optimal until T8.

### Slot Positioning

- Slot position only matters in **Species War** to minimize losses.
- You can't directly control slot assignment — it's organized by power (lowest power = first slot).
- Example: A Sanctum Warrior (5400 HP) in first slot loses ~40 minions/token vs Frost Drake (3500 HP) at ~60 minions/token. Over 7 tokens: 280 vs 420 lost.

### Minion Ratings

- Rating is the general benchmark but **not perfectly correlated** with strength — snail power doesn't directly translate.
- Compare minions **without equipment** for a true comparison.
- Dragons may appear 200-300 rating higher than angels/zombies, but that's often due to equipped gear. Unequipped comparisons may shift priorities.

## Skins

- Skins are **crucial** — naked minions should be replaced if possible.
- **Orange skins > Blue skins** — the stat difference scales with your strength.
- The gap between orange and blue skins widens as you get stronger, making orange skins increasingly important.

## Minion Type Tier Summary

| Minion | Species War | Minion Sim | Notes |
|--------|------------|------------|-------|
| Demon | Great (ATK + Rush) | Good | High attack power |
| Mutant | Great (ATK + Rush) | Good | Strong at T8 |
| Zombie | Good (HP tank) | Great (HP extends rounds) | Best non-dragon at T8 |
| Angel | Good (HP tank) | Great (HP extends rounds) | High survivability |
| Dragon | OK | OK | Not optimal until T8 |

## Key Takeaways

- Prioritize form % gear over non-form gear for each minion type
- Focus rocket cabin upgrades on your primary minion type's cabin
- Demon/Mutant for Species War (ATK/Rush), Angels/Zombies for Minion Sim (HP/survival)
- Dragons are not worth using until T8
- Orange skins are significantly better than blue — the gap scales with power
- Slot position only matters for Species War casualty reduction
- Compare minions unequipped for true strength assessment
- Never use Hulc MK unless running Demon
