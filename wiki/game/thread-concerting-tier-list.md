## Key Takeaways

*   **Top Tier Concerting:** Relics like Maat Sands (Quantum Foam), Particle Collider (Time Machine AFFCT), and those boosting Clone Speed, Refinement Speed, Glorium, Amber, and Protolith output are considered highly valuable.
*   **B-Tad Generation:** Multiple relics across different tiers contribute to B-Tad output, including Particle Collider, Golden Wheat, Faust, Das Kapital, Boat of St. Peter, Portrait of Guevara, Prometheus Torch, Maya Slate, and Book of the Dead.
*   **Paid vs. Free Options:** Some powerful concerting effects are tied to paid packs (e.g., Black Hole Radiation, Temple Pediment, Golden Revival Stele, Sanger Sequencer), while others are obtainable through gameplay.
*   **Specialized Benefits:** Certain relics offer highly specific benefits, such as Lotto Orb upgrades, Minion Sim Calculation boosts, or Divine Ruins currency chance, which may become more relevant depending on player focus.
*   **Uncertainty in New Content:** Some newer additions, particularly those related to Divine Ruins, have effects and relic associations that are still being confirmed by the community.
