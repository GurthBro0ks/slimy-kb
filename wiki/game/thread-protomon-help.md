# Thread Protomon Help

## Protomon General Strategy

Protomon is a recurring event with a weekly cycle, culminating in a special Kaleido week once a month. Understanding this cycle and optimizing your runs is key to progression.

### Weekly Cycle & Steele Levels

The general recommendation for Protomon is to aim for Steele Level 5 each week. This allows for consistent progress without overspending resources.

*   **Kaleido Week:** Every fourth week is a "Kaleido" week. During this week, it's highly recommended to push for Steele Level 15 or even further if possible, as Kaleido offers unique and valuable rewards that are generally considered superior to standard gumball rewards.
*   **Cycle Pattern:** The typical cycle is 5/5/5/Kaleido (e.g., Level 5, Level 5, Level 5, Level 15+ on Kaleido week).
*   **Personalized Levels:** If you are already comfortably clearing a higher Steele level (e.g., Level 10), you can adjust your weekly target accordingly (e.g., 10/5/5/Kaleido).
*   **Energy Requirements:** Each run requires a certain amount of energy. Refer to in-game guides or community resources for specific energy calculations.

### Protomon Run Optimization

During your Protomon runs, strategic choices can significantly impact your progression.

*   **Event Choices:** When presented with event options, prioritize selecting choices that grant "extra protomon stuff." These boosts can help you go further in your run. Some events even guarantee 2 protomon, which are highly beneficial.
*   **Avoid Steele Refreshes:** Generally, do not spend Steele on refreshes for Protomon spins. The only exception might be during your very last run of the week if you are just short of enough Steele to start a new run.
*   **Illusionist Event:** If you encounter the Illusionist, do not waste Steele to grab the two cards. Simply take the free option.

### Protomon Banning

Banning protomon helps refine the pool of available protomon, increasing your chances of getting stronger ones.

*   **Auto-Select:** Utilize the auto-select feature for banning.
*   **Recommended Bans:** Ban all Blue, Purple S, and SS tier protomon. This ensures the system focuses on higher-tier options.
*   **Keeps Best 6:** The system will automatically keep the 6 best protomon in each category after bans.
*   **Energy Recovery:** If you are low on energy but close to starting a new run (e.g., 1350 energy when 1800 is needed), you can manually remove bans. Each ban removed grants 10 energy. Start by removing bans from SS tier and continue until you have enough energy.

## Apostle Cheesing (Protomon Area)

"Cheesing" apostles refers to defeating them with specific setups to progress faster than natural power growth.

*   **Requirements:**
    *   **T40 Lubu:** A fully upgraded T40 Lubu realm trait is generally considered a prerequisite.
    *   **Attack Stat:** Approximately 720,000 attack is often cited, though some players suggest 620,000 might be sufficient for certain apostles.
    *   **Draz:** Having Draz (a specific partner or gear) is highly recommended as it facilitates critical hits, which are crucial for cheesing.
*   **Progression vs. Cheesing:** For players currently around 580,000 attack and facing apostles like "Belt the Bone Swordsman" (which grants one devotee slot), cheesing might not be the most efficient path. Natural progression through accumulating Glory for "rush" clears can often be faster than gaining the significant attack increase needed for cheesing.
*   **Glory Rush Targets:**
    *   **Immortal Ape King:** Requires 490,000 Glory to rush. This apostle unlocks 3 additional devotee slots.
    *   **Last Apostle:** The very last apostle requires 522,836 Glory to rush. There are no specific additional rewards for clearing this final apostle, so it can be completed at your leisure.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Optimize Weekly Runs:** Follow a 5/5/5/Kaleido cycle, pushing for Level 15+ on Kaleido week for superior rewards.
*   **Strategic Choices:** Prioritize "extra protomon stuff" in events and avoid spending Steele on refreshes or Illusionist cards.
*   **Efficient Banning:** Use auto-select to ban all Blue, Purple S, and SS protomon, and leverage ban removal for emergency energy.
*   **Apostle Cheesing is Niche:** Cheesing apostles requires high stats (T40 Lubu, ~720k attack, Draz) and may not be necessary or faster than natural Glory progression for all players, especially for later apostles.
*   **Glory for Later Apostles:** Focus on accumulating Glory to rush "Immortal Ape King" (490k Glory) for significant devotee slot unlocks, rather than solely relying on attack for cheesing.
