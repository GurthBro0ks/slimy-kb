# Thread Biozilla Relics

## Biozilla Relic Optimization Guide

This guide compiles player recommendations for optimizing relic setups for various Biozillas, focusing on maximizing stats and efficiency. General advice includes prioritizing certain relics to 5-stars and understanding slot priorities.

### General Relic Tips

*   Many recurring relics are highly valuable; aim to get these to at least 5 stars.
*   Good relics can be Refined (RF) for specific wars or general use.
*   Enuma Eilish is considered a top-tier relic.
*   For Tech and Art slots, the affection number is less critical than for other slots. Fill these with the best available relics that provide useful stats.

### Biozilla Specific Relic Setups

#### Dung Beetle Biozilla

*   **Fame Slot:** Celestial Mechanics (if 5-star).

#### Goldfish Biozilla

*   Specific setups exist for "Mine" (presumably P2W/optimized) and "F2P" versions, though specific relic names for these were not detailed in the text.

#### Clam Biozilla

*   Specific setups exist for "Mine" and "F2P" versions.
*   **Civ Slot:** Crime and Punishment (if 5-star).
*   **Art Slot:** Harem or Black Body (use if Crime and Punishment is in the Civ slot).

#### Hamster Biozilla

*   Specific setups exist for "Mine" and "F2P" versions.
*   **Fame Slot:** UN Forest (if 5-star).
*   **Revision:** R'lyeh is a highly recommended relic to fill in any missing slots, particularly in Art or Tech, while maintaining high Civ and Faith affection. It is beneficial for both P2W and F2P setups.

#### Mantis Biozilla

*   This Biozilla is noted as being "very P2W for good stuff."
*   Specific setups exist for "Mine" and "F2P" versions.
*   **Fame Slot:** Pillar of Creation (if 5-star).
*   **Tech and Art Slots:** These slots are less critical for affection numbers; fill them with any suitable relics you possess.
*   **General Filler:** A purple relic offering 4.5% ATK is a good option if you need attack and do not have Qin Bronze with at least purple stamps.

### Relic Specific Discussions

#### Ivan the Terrible

*   **Comparison:** Ivan the Terrible (9% Rush/Def) vs. Harem Wall Painting (6% Rush).
*   **Placement in Civ Slot:** Using Ivan the Terrible in the Civ slot is generally not recommended. While it offers 9% Rush, it has low Civ affection and results in a significant loss of Attack (approximately 3.8% ATK) compared to other options. The gain of 3% Rush is often not worth the ATK sacrifice.
*   **Recommended Use:** Ivan the Terrible is better suited for "purge stuff" due to its high Rush percentage. If a player is missing a relic for the Tech slot, Ivan the Terrible can be a viable option there.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Prioritize 5-Star Relics:** Many recurring and powerful relics should be upgraded to 5 stars for optimal performance.
*   **Slot Specificity Matters:** Fame and Civ slots often have specific, high-impact relic recommendations (e.g., Celestial Mechanics, Crime and Punishment, UN Forest, Pillar of Creation).
*   **Flexibility in Art/Tech:** Art and Tech slots are more flexible; focus on filling them with relics that provide useful stats rather than strictly chasing affection numbers.
*   **R'lyeh as a Versatile Filler:** R'lyeh is a strong general-purpose relic, especially useful for filling gaps in Hamster Biozilla setups while maintaining key affections.
*   **Strategic Relic Choices:** Evaluate relics like Ivan the Terrible carefully; while powerful in certain stats (e.g., Rush), their overall impact on other crucial stats (like Attack or Civ affection) should be considered for optimal placement.
