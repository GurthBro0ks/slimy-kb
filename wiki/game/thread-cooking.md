# Thread Cooking

## Cooking System Guide

The Cooking system in Super Snail allows players to prepare dishes, fulfill orders, and earn rewards. It's designed as a long-term progression system.

### How to Unlock

To unlock the Cooking system:
1.  Dominate Athena in the Time Rift (9th realm).
2.  Clown Express will visit your mailbox.
3.  Pay Clown Express 1,000 W-tads.
4.  The cooking area will then be available in your dormitory.

### General Gameplay Loop

The Cooking system is a marathon, not a sprint. If you find yourself unable to progress reliably, it's often best to wait for the next day.

Your primary activities should include:
*   **Shop Purchases:** Prioritize buying recipes and skills from the shop. Avoid buying ingredients initially.
*   **Upgrades:** Upgrade your delivery service and cooking utensils.
*   **Dish Development:** Develop and upgrade new dishes.
*   **Order Fulfillment:** Fulfill orders of any quality, or aim for sales targets, preferably orange quality.
*   **Rotation:** Once orders/targets for a particular dish are completed, consider dropping it for a new one, unless you have a significant excess of its ingredients.

### Dish Development

It is optimal to stop trying to develop a dish if you can consistently hit "Perfect heat" during cooking. At this point, you should see a progression chance of about 15-20%. This percentage may change with future updates.

### Unlocking the Mutant Partner

To unlock the Mutant Partner:
1.  Have your Mutant at Tier 8.
2.  Unlock the second delivery service.
3.  Find the octopus-looking dish.
4.  Max the octopus dish to orange quality.
5.  Complete an order for the octopus dish.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

*   **Stuck on Dish Development:** If you can't develop any more dishes, especially after unlocking partner skills, simply wait for the next day's shop refresh.
*   **Lack of Coins:** If you urgently need coins for essential purchases (like delivery upgrades or skills), you may consider cooking purple quality dishes. However, try not to overdo this, as every dish not cooked for an order or at orange quality represents a lost opportunity for optimal progression.

### Mega Stew

Mega Stew serves as an excess ingredient sink. If your storage is reaching its limit, it's acceptable to cook some purple or blue ingredients in the Mega Stew. The median price of ingredients cooked this way will be slightly above blue quality. It's unlikely you will efficiently use all blue and purple ingredients otherwise.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Marathon, Not a Sprint:** Approach Cooking as a long-term progression system; waiting for daily resets is often optimal.
*   **Prioritize Upgrades & Skills:** Focus on buying recipes/skills, and upgrading delivery/utensils over initial ingredient purchases.
*   **Strategic Dish Management:** Develop dishes, fulfill orders (especially orange quality), and rotate completed dishes.
*   **Mutant Partner Requirements:** Unlocking the Mutant Partner requires a T8 Mutant, second delivery service, and maxing/ordering the specific octopus dish.
*   **Mega Stew for Excess:** Use Mega Stew to manage excess ingredients, especially if storage is full, but don't rely on it for primary progression.
