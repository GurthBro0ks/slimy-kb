# Thread Rift Guide

## Rift Overview

The Rift is a challenging new exploration area unlocked after defeating the final Apostle of Bharata. It introduces new mechanics, apostles, minions, and significant rewards, but also requires strategic planning for optimal progression.

## Initial Strategy and Unlocking Lotto 2

Upon unlocking the Rift, it is recommended to **not use your speedups immediately**. Instead, focus on speeding up only the first two areas to quickly unlock **Lotto 2**. Lotto 2 is obtained by dominating the second Apostle in the Rift. This Apostle has relatively low HP and can often be defeated with farm gear and Lady Liberty, even if you've just barely beaten the last Apostle in Bharata. The third Apostle, however, presents a significant power jump.

It is advised to save speedups for a key feature known as "Rift Partners" (also called Drifters), which provide substantial buffs like 15% move speed.

## General Exploration

Rift exploration is structured into several "Time-spaces," each mirroring one of the existing 8 realms. At launch, there are 8 Time-spaces available. Each Time-space is further divided into 5 areas, similar to regular realms, complete with their own Apostles.

The Rift introduces a total of 40 new Apostles and 176 new minions, offering a substantial amount of toner and other rewards.

## Exploration Loot

Based on Chinese server estimations, the Rift offers significantly increased rewards compared to previous realms:
*   Approximately **2x more B-Tads** than Bharata.
*   Approximately **3x more cells** than Hellas.

The Rift drops high quantities of both **Gear Chests** and **Fungus Chests**. It also drops Time, Evo, Tool, and all three Divine Chests, though in much lower quantities. Additionally, players can obtain both Offering and Gene speedups, estimated at around 2.5 hours, though it's unclear if this is per run or per day.

## Intel

The Rift features an extensive Intel tree, with the first page alone containing 283 upgrades that cost over 175 million Intel to max out. Lady Liberty is highly recommended for her Intel boost to accelerate progression.

### Intel Upgrade Priority (General)

A general upgrade order for the Intel tree is as follows:
*   **Upgrades 1-4**: Boosts to travel speed, cards, exploration tiles, and food capacity (food capacity becomes more critical in the Rift).
*   **Upgrade 6**: Increases damage.
*   **Upgrade 7**: Increases maps dropped (leading to more loot).
*   **Upgrades 5, 8-15**: Provide buffs to exploration tiles and general exploration loot.
*   **Upgrades 16-19**: These are very expensive, with 50 levels each, and increase your HARD stats (Hit, Attack, Resistance, Defense) for defeating new Rift Apostles.

### Intel Upgrade Priority (First Tree Specific)

For the initial Intel tree, a specific path is recommended:
1.  Max out "7" at the top.
2.  Then, put 1 level into the bottom tile.
3.  Max out "8" at the top.
4.  Then, put another level into the bottom tile.
5.  Max out "9" at the top.
6.  Then, put another level into the bottom tile.

## Rift Partners (Drifters)

Rift Partners, also known as Drifters, are a crucial feature providing massive stat buffs (e.g., 15% move speed). They are obtained by dominating various Apostles throughout the Rift.

### Partner Upgrades and Traits

*   **Upgrading Partners**: Partners can be upgraded as materials become available. The first upgrade materials are unique to specific partners or their groups, while subsequent materials are shared among all Drifters and tend to be more plentiful.
*   **Trait Upgrades**: Trait upgrades are more nuanced. It's generally advised to focus on upgrading traits if you are stuck on a specific challenge (e.g., a dance battle) and are significantly below the recommended power level. Prioritize upgrading traits that are SSS or SS rank for that particular gimmick.

### Partner Acquisition and Priority

While initial priority lists for Drifters exist, it's important to note that many of the most powerful Drifters (e.g., #1-5 on some lists) are acquired much later in Rift progression. Therefore, early-game priority may differ from long-term goals.

For comprehensive information on Drifters, including acquisition, stats, and detailed upgrade priorities, refer to community-maintained resources such as **Wonderwilly's Drifter Wonderguide spreadsheet**.

## Exploration Gear

New exploration gear is available in the Rift:
*   **White through Purple gear**: Can be purchased from the card exchange.
*   **Orange item**: Drops from Apostle 27, which is typically too powerful for early-game players.
*   **Red Rift explore item**: Once obtained, this special item allows you to combine all of your existing realm exploration gear into a single, powerful item, streamlining your gear management.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Unlock Lotto 2 ASAP**: Defeat Rift Apostle 2 quickly using minimal speedups and Lady Liberty.
*   **Save Speedups**: Hold off on extensive speedup use until Rift Partners (Drifters) are fully available, especially for their valuable move speed buffs.
*   **Prioritize Intel**: Use Lady Liberty for Intel boosts and follow the recommended Intel upgrade paths to enhance exploration speed, loot, and combat stats.
*   **Drifters are Key**: Understand that Drifters provide significant power boosts; refer to community guides for optimal acquisition and upgrade strategies, especially for traits.
*   **New Gear & Loot**: Expect substantial increases in B-Tads and cells, and work towards the Red Rift explore item to consolidate your exploration gear.
