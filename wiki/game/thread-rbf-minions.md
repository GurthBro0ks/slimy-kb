# Thread Rbf Minions

## Minion Strategy & Lineup

Minion setup is crucial for both Minion Sim and War. The optimal lineup depends on your current progression and available T8 minions.

### General Principles

*   **Prioritize T8 Minions:** Always aim to convert T7 minions to T8 rather than cloning T8 from scratch. Converting T7 to T8 is significantly faster (e.g., 1 day 14 hours vs. 4 days 17 hours for 5 T8 from scratch).
*   **Fastest Clone Time:** Identify your fastest T7-to-T8 conversion time (e.g., Demon, Ironball, Angel) and use that minion type as a base for conversions, then swap to the desired minion type.
