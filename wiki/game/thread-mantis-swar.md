# Thread Mantis Swar

## Mantis Swar Event Guide

The Mantis Swar is an upcoming chronicle event where players take on the role of Wulun Zun on their journey to mastery. The event involves accruing points through travel and practicing skills, culminating in PvP tournaments.

### Event Mechanics

*   **Player Character:** You will play as Wulun Zun.
*   **Progression:** Gain points by traveling and practicing skills.
*   **Practice Rewards:** Practicing skills can randomly yield:
    *   Skills (passive or active abilities)
    *   Martial Arts
    *   More Bags (presumably inventory or reward bags)
    *   Mantis Chests

### Martial Arts

There are three types of Martial Arts available:
*   **Sword**
*   **Fist**
*   **Hidden Weapon**

These Martial Arts are believed to operate in a Rock-Paper-Scissors equilibrium:
*   **Sword** beats **Fist**
*   **Fist** beats **Hidden Weapons**
*   **Hidden Weapons** beats **Sword**

Training in these Martial Arts will grant you various skills, which can be either passive or active.

### PvP Tournaments

The event features PvP tournaments with varying point rewards:
*   **Orange Tier:** 200,000 points
*   **Purple Tier:** 100,000 points
*   **Blue Tier:** 50,000 points

**Player Strategy:**
It is anticipated that high-spending players ("Whales") will compete in the Orange and possibly Purple tiers, while other players will likely participate in the Blue tier.

**Tournament Schedule:**
Similar to the Hamster Swar, there will be a specific timeframe for setting up and participating in the tournaments before results are finalized.

## Key Takeaways

*   The Mantis Swar event casts players as Wulun Zun, focusing on skill mastery and PvP.
*   Progression involves traveling for points and practicing skills to earn new abilities, martial arts, and rewards.
*   Three Martial Arts (Sword, Fist, Hidden Weapon) exist in a Rock-Paper-Scissors dynamic.
*   PvP tournaments are tiered (Orange, Purple, Blue) with different point payouts, influencing player participation strategies.
*   The event has a defined setup and participation window, similar to other Swar events.
