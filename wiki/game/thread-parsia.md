## Hero Strategies

Specific hero names are not provided due to game updates, but their descriptions and roles are detailed below:

*   **Recommended Heroes:**
    *   **Blue Archer Girl:** Boosts damage to all heroes and other stats.
    *   **Stone-looking Priest:** Stuns enemies.
    *   **Cat Warrior:** Stuns enemies.

*   **General Combat Strategy:**
    *   The primary strategy revolves around **spamming energy attacks**. Each energy attack used stacks damage, leading to increased output over time.
    *   **Stun Management:** The Stone-looking Priest's stun ability is particularly potent as it can stun an entire wave of enemies that have entered the field. It's advised to sometimes save this stun for opportune moments.

*   **Hero Usage Scenarios:**
    *   **Using all three heroes:** When all three hero slots are available, the recommended lineup is the Blue Archer Girl, the Stone-looking Priest, and the Cat Warrior.
    *   **Using a single hero:** If only one hero can be deployed, the **Stone-looking Priest** is the top recommendation due to its wave-stunning capability.

## Key Takeaways

*   A specific set of 8 relics is recommended for Parsia, including "Tyrant's Game" and "The Book of a Thousand Laws."
*   The core combat strategy involves spamming energy attacks to continuously stack damage.
*   The "Stone-looking Priest" is highly valued for its ability to stun entire enemy waves, making it the top choice if only one hero can be deployed.
*   When three heroes are available, a combination of the "Blue Archer Girl" (damage/stat boost), "Stone-looking Priest" (stun), and "Cat Warrior" (stun) is recommended.
