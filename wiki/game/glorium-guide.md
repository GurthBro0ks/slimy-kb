# Glorium Maximization Guide

Step-by-step strategy to maximize glorium income using priest/devotee skill point allocation and biome fervor management.

## Step 1: Unlock All Second Relic Slots

1. Save **250 glorium** before starting (each biome unlock costs 50).
2. Reset priest and devotee slots for all biomes.
3. Pour all priest/devotee points into **one biome** until it reaches **30k fervor**.
4. Unlock the second relic slot and place your best available relic in it.
   - Use relics that boost glorium/affect, as long as they don't give medals for a museum.
5. Reset the priest/devotee of that biome and move to the next.
6. Repeat until all biomes have their second relic slot unlocked.
7. Once unlocked, you keep the slot permanently — even if the biome drops below 30k afterward.

> The second relic slot alone provides roughly **20% more glorium per biome**, even without focusing on boost stats.

## Step 2: Min-Max Glorium Farming (38k Fervor Strategy)

This is the core farming loop — treat it like rift where you run farm setup 99% of the time and swap to push every so often.

1. Reset all biomes' priest and devotee skills (not the protomon itself).
2. Pick a single biome — preferably one with a **glorium boost stamp**.
3. Pour all priest/devotee skill points into that biome until it reaches **38k fervor**.
   - This is a HUGE boost to income — nearly doubles that biome's daily gain due to higher rating.
   - **Stop exactly at 38k if you can** to maximize efficiency.
4. Put remaining points into the next best glorium income biome, also targeting 38k.
5. Continue cascading down your biomes.

**Screenshot tip:** Before resetting, screenshot all your biomes so you know exactly what levels to restore priest/devotee skills to, minimizing waste from mistakes.

## Step 3: Periodic Apostle Push

Every month (or whatever interval works for you):

1. Reset all biomes and redistribute priest/devotee points **as evenly as possible** across all biomes.
   - This maximizes your **Glory stat**, which gives you rush on fights.
2. Push apostles with the high glory/rush to unlock more devotee slots.
3. When you can't push any further, **reset back** to the 38k fervor farming setup.

## How to Reset Fervor

1. Click your **Priest** in a biome.
2. Click the tabs in the top right corner.
3. Reset at the bottom (second option).
4. Alternatively, you can reset just priests to max one at a time, then reset and move down the line.

## Costs

- **50 wtads** per skill reset.
- Full process is roughly **1-2k wtads** at most.
- Relatively cheap for the glorium gains.

## F2P Considerations

- The second relic slot boost comes primarily from the affect boost rather than stamps.
- Worth doing even without lotto 3 or many protomon relics — the 20% boost is significant.

## Terminology

- **Priest** — The lead protomon in each biome (circled in screenshots).
- **Devotees** — The subordinate protomons in each biome.
- Each biome has one priest and several devotees.
- **Fervor** — The resource generated by leveling priest/devotee skills.

## Key Takeaways

- Unlock 2nd relic slots on ALL biomes first (30k fervor per biome, costs 50 glorium each)
- Farm by concentrating priest/devotee points into single biomes to 38k fervor for maximum income
- Push apostles monthly by redistributing points evenly for high Glory stat and rush
- Screenshot biomes before resetting to minimize mistakes
- Reset costs are low (~50 wtads per skill reset, 1-2k total)
- The 20% glorium boost from 2nd relic slots is worth doing even as F2P
- Prioritize biomes with glorium boost stamps for farming focus
