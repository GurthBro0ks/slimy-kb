# Thread Edge Minions

## Minion Lineup Strategy

The primary goal for minion lineup is to maximize Species War damage. This often means focusing on a specific minion type that offers high Attack and Rush stats.

**Recommended Minion Lineup:**
1.  **Demons:** Max out all available Demon minions to their individual caps.
2.  **Tomb Figure:** Convert any remaining non-Demon T8 minions to Tomb Figures until its cap is reached.
3.  **Mummy:** Fill the rest of your minion slots with Mummy minions.

**Minion Conversion:**
*   When you gain more leadership, prioritize cloning your fastest T8 minions and converting them into Mummy minions to fill out your lineup.

## Minion Caps

Minion caps refer to the maximum number of a specific minion type you can field.
*   **General Average:** Approximately 150-170 per minion type.
*   **Specific Examples (Player's Stats):**
    *   Zombie Minions: 193
    *   Demons: 164
    *   Angels: 165
    *   Mummy: 198
*   **Checking Cap:** To determine a minion's cap, try converting other T8 minions into that type using the minion converter. If you cannot convert more, you've reached the cap.

## Gear Recommendations

Gear selection and order are crucial for maximizing Species War bonuses. The recommended setup focuses on boosting Demon stats and providing useful secondary effects.

**Recommended Gear Lineup (in order):**
1.  **Demon % Gear:** +4 enhancement (e.g., Demon Suit, Demon Blade)
2.  **Hulc:** +3 enhancement
3.  **Zombie Mind Control Helmet:** +2 enhancement
4.  **Nethrone:** +1 enhancement (replaces Mutant % gear)
5.  **Mutant Boots:** (no specific enhancement level mentioned, but typically +1 or higher)
6.  **Angel Shield:** (for Simulation benefits, otherwise a strong red gear)

**Important Note on Gear Order:** The order in which relics/gear are equipped significantly affects the "Species War Bonus" stats displayed at the bottom of your combat stats. Ensure you equip them in the recommended sequence.

## Relic Recommendations

Relics provide additional stat boosts for your snail and minions.

**Mantis Relics (Example Player Setup):**
*   12% Damage to Hero
*   24% Attack
*   9% Crit Damage
*   24% Rush
*   9% Crit

**F2P Alternative Relic:**
*   If you do not have the "Art Relic" at purple tier, a "Water Resistance Tech Relic" can be used as an alternative, providing 4.5% Attack.

## General Tips

*   **Species War vs. Simulation:** This minion and gear setup is optimized for Species War damage. It may result in lower performance in Simulation battles.
*   **Biozilla Setups:** For Biozilla-specific setups, refer to the dedicated #biozilla-setups channel.
*   **Minion Stats:** Demon minions generally offer higher Rush and Attack stats compared to Zombie minions, making them a strong choice for Species War.

## Key Takeaways

*   Prioritize **Demon minions** to their cap, then **Tomb Figures**, and finally **Mummy minions** to fill your lineup for optimal Species War performance.
*   The **order of equipped gear** is critical for maximizing Species War bonuses, not just the gear pieces themselves.
*   Focus on gear that boosts **Demon % stats**, along with specific pieces like Hulc, Zombie Mind Control Helmet, Nethrone, Mutant Boots, and Angel Shield.
*   This minion and gear strategy is designed to **increase damage in Species War**, potentially at the cost of Simulation performance.
*   Always check your **minion caps** and convert lower-priority T8 minions to fill out your strongest types as your leadership increases.
