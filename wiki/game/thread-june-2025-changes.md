# Thread June 2025 Changes

## June 2025 Changes Overview

A document titled "Revolutionary Rework Day aka Fred" was shared, outlining significant upcoming changes for June 2025. These changes primarily impact game resources and how certain unlocks are handled.

## Controller and Resource Rework

The "controller" resource is undergoing a significant rework. Key points regarding this change include:

*   **Altar God Unlocks:** For players who have not yet unlocked Altar gods that currently require controllers, these requirements will be updated to utilize the new resources introduced with the rework.
*   **Spending Controllers:** There has been discussion among players about whether to spend existing controllers before the update.
    *   It is **not required** to spend your controllers, and choosing not to will not negatively impact your account.
    *   The recommendation from some players to spend them is based on the belief that the automatic conversion rate of old controllers to the new resources will be inefficient or "garbage."
*   **Golden Controller Relic:** Players are advised to ensure they have acquired the Golden Controller relic, as its status or acquisition method may be affected by the resource rework.

## Key Takeaways

*   June 2025 brings a "Revolutionary Rework" primarily affecting game resources, particularly "controllers."
*   Altar gods that currently require controllers will have their unlock requirements updated to new resources if not yet unlocked.
*   Spending existing controllers before the update is optional; the automatic conversion rate is perceived by some players as inefficient.
*   Players should ensure they have obtained the Golden Controller relic.
