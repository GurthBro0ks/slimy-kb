# Thread T7 Rituals

## T7 Rituals Overview

T7 Rituals are a set of challenges that players can undertake to earn rewards, primarily B-tads. Each ritual corresponds to a specific snail form (Demon, Mech, Zombie, Angelic, Mutant) and has unique prerequisites. Completing these rituals is a significant step in progression, often requiring substantial resource investment or combat power.

All T7 Rituals, upon completion, reward **100 B-tads**.

## Demon Ritual

The Demon T7 Ritual is generally considered straightforward for players reaching this stage.

*   **Requirement:** Complete Manhunt Level 3.
*   **Difficulty:** Described by players as "free" or easily beaten, implying most players will have already completed Manhunt Lv.3 by the time they access T7 Rituals.

## Mech Ritual

The Mech T7 Ritual involves a unique partner quest and a significant combat challenge.

*   **Requirement:** Complete the "Silicon Life" quest.
*   **Combat Requirement:** Players report a fight within the Mech path that requires approximately **160,000 Attack** to beat. This is likely a step either during or after the Silicon Life quest, leading up to the ritual's completion.

### Silicon Life Quest (Prerequisite for Mech Ritual)

The "Silicon Life" quest introduces a Mecha Snail partner who appears in your mailbox and requests various resources. This quest is known to be a "long one" due to the cumulative resource demands.

**Silicon Life Quest Requirements:**

*   Provide 1000 Green Cells
*   Provide 1000 Blue Cells
*   Provide 1000 Purple Cells
*   Provide 1000 Orange Cells
*   Provide 1000 Red Cells
*   Provide 1000 Black Cells
*   Provide 2000 Refine Speedup
*   Provide 500 Robots
*   Provide 100 WTAD (World Tree Ancient Dragon)

## Zombie, Angelic, and Mutant Rituals

These three T7 Rituals share a similar, somewhat counter-intuitive, prerequisite structure.

*   **Zombie T7 Ritual Requirement:** Complete the Zombie T8 Ritual.
*   **Angelic T7 Ritual Requirement:** Complete the Angelic T8 Ritual.
*   **Mutant T7 Ritual Requirement:** Complete the Mutant T8 Ritual.

This means that to unlock and complete the T7 ritual for these forms, players must first complete the corresponding T8 ritual.

## Key Takeaways

*   All T7 Rituals reward 100 B-tads upon completion.
*   The Demon T7 Ritual requires Manhunt Lv.3 and is considered easy.
*   The Mech T7 Ritual requires completing the "Silicon Life" partner quest, which demands a large quantity of various cells, speedups, robots, and WTAD.
*   A combat encounter in the Mech path requires approximately 160,000 Attack.
*   The Zombie, Angelic, and Mutant T7 Rituals uniquely require completion of their respective T8 Rituals as a prerequisite.
