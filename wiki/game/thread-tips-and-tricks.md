# Thread Tips And Tricks

This article compiles various tips and tricks for Super Snail, covering aspects from talent tree optimization to partner leveling, devour strategies, and advanced research.

## T8 Ritual Talent Tree Optimization

This strategy focuses on maximizing Glorium and Protomon resource output during T8 rituals by temporarily changing your talent tree setup.

**Stone's Strategy:**
1.  **Before Ritual Start**: Ensure your talent tree is set up for research speed and long-term progression.
2.  **After Ritual Starts**: Change your talent tree to prioritize Glorium and Protomon resource output.
    *   Go down the left side with purple nodes only.
    *   Once you reach 14f, switch to orange nodes only (do not do the purple node there).
    *   On 18f, do the purple node first.
    *   On 19f, max that node.
    *   Then, max the 18f orange node.
3.  **Crucial Tip**: **DO NOT USE GENE RESEARCH SPEEDUPS** while in this resource-focused tree. This allows you to benefit from the increased resource income for as long as possible.
4.  **Before Ritual Ends**: With approximately one day left on the ritual timer, swap back to your original talent tree setup (with 6f and 12f purple and orange nodes completed). This will reduce the remaining ritual time by about 7.5 hours. Do not wait until the ritual is completely finished to swap back.

**Player Feedback/Considerations (Genesia):**
*   This strategy might not be worth it if you don't have a significant amount of orange talent points to invest.
*   One player reported only gaining a single level in the Glorium talent, while losing benefits from other nodes like time machine, clone speedup, leadership, and soul offering.
*   The actual Glorium gain can vary significantly based on your current production. A 15% increase might be +300 Glorium/day for a high-level player, but only +100 Glorium/day for others, making the trade-off less appealing.
*   It's important to assess if the temporary loss of other crucial stats (leadership, minion stats, offering, clone speedup, time machine) for potentially two months is justified by your Glorium/Protomon gains.

## Partner Leveling Efficiency

To save manuals when leveling partners to Level 30:
*   Utilize the 3-decor bonus for a partner. This bonus allows you to save 1,400 manuals per form, totaling 7,000 manuals saved per partner.
*   With the 3-decor bonus active, you only need to level the partner's specialized form (the green one) to Level 100, and all other forms to Level 98.
*   This method works with green, blue, and purple buys. Orange buys are not strictly necessary for this manual-saving trick, but are good for maximizing decor rating.
*   **Recommended Partners**: Prioritize maxing Toraemon first, followed by Lee Gi-na.

## Devour Optimization

**Devour Filter Settings:**
Adjust your Devour filter to provide more options that might otherwise be filtered out due to lower scores. This can lead to discovering better devours.
*   Set your filter options to include:
    *   Snail All Element Crit % / All Minion Atk %
    *   Paralyze Enemy % / Leadership
    *   Chance to Trigger Stonehide % / All Minion Hp %

**Desired Devour Stats:**
When evaluating devours, prioritize the following stats:
*   **Bottom Stat Boost**: Leadership/Minion % is generally the primary focus.
*   **Top Stat Combinations**:
    *   Leadership / Paralyze
    *   Minion HP / DEF (HP is generally preferred) with Stonehide as a top stat.
    *   Minion ATK / Rush with Crit Dmg / Element Crit for the last three.

## T9 Research Strategy

T9 research is a significant time investment, comparable to completing all six T8 forms. To maximize value:
1.  **Prioritize Best Minion**: Start by researching your best minion to T9 first.
2.  **Leadership Nodes**: After completing your best minion's T9, focus on completing all leadership nodes.
    *   **Dragon Leadership Node**: Prioritize the Dragon leadership node first among the five, as it increases the Dragon minion cap by 25 per Dragon minion at max.
3.  **Second Best Minion**: After all leadership nodes are done, move to your second-best minion from T8.
4.  **Form Research**: Then, begin T9 form research for your second-best non-Dragon minion.
5.  **Subsequent Minions**: Mecha is often recommended as the third priority due to its Golem benefits, but subsequent choices can be based on personal preference.

## Vehicle Decor Tips

For vehicle decor, specifically regarding the "second green color" option:
*   Each week, allocate 100 bonds into all stock options on the start day.
*   Invest the remaining bonds into your desired investment.
*   Upgrade the vehicle decor to the "second green color" tier, as this tier is typically free and does not cost auto chips.

## Robot Allocation for Efficiency

Optimize your robot placement across cabins:
1.  **Clone Speed Cabin**: Remove all purple and orange robots from other cabins. Place all orange robots, then all purple robots, into the first cabin to maximize clone speed.
2.  **Refine Cabin**: Place the next best remaining robots into the refine cabin.
3.  **Other Cabins**: Distribute the rest of your robots into other cabins as needed.

## Second Civilization Steele Inlay Setup

For the Second Civilization Steele, which has two centers, each providing +8 per outside inlay:
*   **First Steele Center**: Place Quantum Radio here. It requires 6 inlays to get +18.
*   **Second Steele Center**: Place Tokamak and Collider here. These require 5 outside inlays for an extra 15 affinity when maxed.

## Key Takeaways

*   **T8 Rituals**: Consider a temporary talent tree swap for resource gain, but weigh the trade-offs in other stats, especially if your resource income is not high.
*   **Partner Leveling**: Utilize the 3-decor bonus to save 7,000 manuals per partner by leveling forms to 98/100.
*   **Devour Strategy**: Adjust filters and prioritize Leadership/Minion % bottom stats, paired with specific top stats like All Element Crit, Paralyze, or Stonehide.
*   **T9 Research**: Follow a strategic path focusing on your best minion first, then all leadership nodes (starting with Dragon), before moving to other minions.
*   **Resource Management**: Optimize robot allocation for clone speed and refine, and use vehicle decor tips to gain bonuses without spending premium currency.
