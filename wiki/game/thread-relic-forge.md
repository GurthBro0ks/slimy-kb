# Thread Relic Forge

## Relic Forge Recommendations

This guide compiles recommendations for which relics to prioritize in the Relic Forge (RF), focusing on efficiency and long-term gains. The advice is sourced from experienced players within the Super Snail Discord community.

### General Relic Forge Principles

*   **UNISPARK T4 is the highest priority** for Dragon evolution.
*   **Avoid flat stat relics** like Ragnarok. Relics that offer percentage-based farming boosts or cost reductions provide significantly more value over time.
*   **Skyweb Hub and King Dag (Awakened)** are needed for the Murika domain, but **do not use Relic Forge on them** as it is considered a waste of resources.
*   **Origin of Species and Soulstone** are important as they have final mirage partners.
*   **Phoenix Feather** is required to obtain the Scales of Anubis, but you must first defeat Anubis (4.5M power).

### Top Priority Relics

*   **Magellan:** Prioritize to 5\* with max stamps.

### Relics by Category

#### Time Machine Output

*   **Particle Collider:** Prioritize to 5\* with max stamps (found in Wish Box Set 3). Awakening the Particle Collider provides extra production in the farm and contributes 1/3 of the 16% time output resonance.
*   **Artificial Blackhole:** Prioritize to Awakened (found in Wish Box Set 5).

#### Travel Speed

Travel speed is increasingly important due to the length of Rift content.

*   **Somersault Cloud:** Prioritize to 6\* (found in Wish Box Sets 3 and 5).
*   **Voyager 1:** Prioritize to 6\* (found in Wish Box Set 1).

#### Btad Output

*   **Golden Wheat:** Prioritize to Awakened (found in Wish Box Set 4). Provides Time Machine and Btad output, with the best stamps for the Tadpole Pond.
*   **Lotus Chalice:** Prioritize to Awakened. Good at 5\* for stamps, and awakening contributes to the 16% time machine output resonance.
*   **Faust:** Prioritize to Awakened. Considered the "Artificial Blackhole of Btads."

#### Gene Research Speed

*   **Double Helix DNA:** Prioritize to Awakened.
*   **The Analects:** Prioritize to Awakened (found in Wish Box Set 1).

#### Soul Offering Speed

*   **Homuwu Ding:** Prioritize to Awakened (found in Wish Box Set 2).
*   **Prometheus Torch:** Prioritize to Awakened.

#### Cloning Speed

*   **Origin of Species:** Prioritize to Awakened (found in Wish Box Set 5). As a bonus, awakening this relic grants a new partner.

#### Leadership

*   **Trojan Horse:** Prioritize to Awakened.
*   **Terracotta Soldier:** Prioritize to Awakened.

#### Refine Speed

*   **Science Magazine Vol 1:** Prioritize to Awakened. This is a low priority initially but becomes an insanely good relic. It is recommended to save refine speedups until this relic is awakened, especially after all your cabins are at least blue quality.

#### General Minion Boosting

*   **Atlantis Crystal:** Prioritize to 6\*.
*   **Orphic Egg:** Prioritize to 6\*.

### Wish Box Sets Explained

"Wish Box Sets" refer to special event rewards where players can choose one relic from a predetermined set. These sets are categorized (e.g., Set 1, Set 2) and may be offered as event rewards in future game updates, potentially replacing specific relic choices in some events.

### Other Noteworthy Relics

*   **Relativity Script:** Mentioned as a personal favorite.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Prioritize Magellan and UnisparK T4** for early game progression and Dragon evolution.
*   **Focus on percentage-based buffs** (e.g., Time Machine Output, Btad Output, Research Speed) over flat stat increases.
*   **Awakening specific relics** like Artificial Blackhole, Golden Wheat, and Origin of Species provides significant long-term benefits.
*   **Museum Medal relics** are crucial for resource generation; aim for 5\* with max stamps on recommended items.
*   **Strategic use of Refine Speedups** by saving them for Science Magazine Vol 1's awakening can optimize resource management.
