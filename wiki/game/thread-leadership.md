# Thread Leadership

## Leadership Breakdown

Leadership is a crucial stat in Super Snail, affecting various aspects of gameplay. Here's a detailed breakdown of how to increase your Leadership:

### Base Leadership

*   Everyone starts with a base of **20 Leadership**.

### Gene Research (T4 Forms)

For each T4 form, you can gain +10 Leadership from the middle node of its research tree.
*   **Total:** Up to **60 Leadership** (for 6 forms).
*   **Achievability:** Dragon T4 is typically achievable around 1.8M to 2M power.

### Collectors Talent

The right side of the Collector's Talent tree offers Leadership bonuses at odd levels.
*   **1st Level:** +10 Leadership (requires 5 blue talent points).
*   **3rd Level:** +10 Leadership (requires 10 blue talent points and 5 purple/orange talent points to unlock).
*   **5th Level:** +10 Leadership (requires 15 blue talent points and 10 purple/orange talent points to unlock).
*   **Recommendation:** Aim for at least the 1st and 3rd levels early on.
*   **Total:** Up to **30 Leadership**.

### Relics

Leadership gained from relics varies based on their star level. The following lists provide examples of relics that grant Leadership. Values assume at least 3 stars for SSS relics.

**SSS Relics (Easy to Get)**
*   **Trojan Horse:** +5/7/9/12 Leadership (Source: Relic Fragment (RF) shop, Fame Wish, Lotto).
*   **Energy Missile:** +5/7/9/12 Leadership (Source: Lucky Coin reward during Wish Event).
*   **Species Plantarum:** +5/7/9/12 Leadership (Source: Observatory during Offering Event - note: this can be tricky to navigate, ask for help if needed).
*   **New Leadership Relic:** A new relic granting Leadership was added to the Relic Fragment (RF) shop as of March 2024.

**Green Relics**
*   **Father's Photo:** +3 Leadership (Source: Zap the pig with incense 10 times).
*   **The Astrology:** +3 Leadership (Source: Observatory during Offering Event - note: you need to buy the first two green relics before this one becomes available).
*   **Alexander Anabasis:** +15 Leadership (Source: Hellas - requires dominating the Last Bharata Apostle).

**Relic Totals:**
*   **Early Minimum:** ~18 Leadership
*   **Late Minimum:** ~36 Leadership
*   **Maximum:** ~57 Leadership (and potentially more with the new RF relic and higher star levels).

### Orange Mushrooms

Eating Orange Mushrooms provides Leadership at specific milestones.
*   **Milestones:** +10 Leadership for each milestone reached at 25, 50, 120, 250, 500 mushrooms eaten.
*   **Early Strategy:** Focus on eating Orange and Green Mushrooms up to around 125. Convert all Purple and Blue Mushrooms to Orange/Green if you don't have enough.
*   **Early Minimum:** +30 Leadership
*   **Maximum:** +50 Leadership (technically unlimited as you can keep eating them).

### Gene Research (T6 Forms)

Each T6 form offers +10 Leadership per level of its middle node, for a total of +30 Leadership per form.
*   **Level 1:** 50,000 Cells
*   **Level 2:** 100,000 Cells
*   **Level 3:** 250,000 Cells
*   **Total:** Up to **180 Leadership** (for 6 forms). This requires a significant amount of cells and time.

---

## Key Takeaways

*   **Diverse Sources:** Leadership can be increased through Gene Research (T4 & T6), Collector's Talent, specific Relics, and Orange Mushroom consumption.
*   **Early Focus:** Prioritize T4 Gene Research, the first few levels of Collector's Talent, and eating Orange Mushrooms up to the 120-125 milestone for quick Leadership gains.
*   **Relic Importance:** Actively pursue Leadership-granting relics from various sources like RF, Wish Events, and the Observatory.
*   **Long-Term Goal:** T6 Gene Research provides the largest single source of Leadership but requires substantial cell investment and time.
*   **Strategic Mushroom Use:** Convert lower-tier mushrooms to Orange/Green to reach Orange Mushroom milestones faster.
