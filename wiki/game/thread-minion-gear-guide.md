# Thread Minion Gear Guide

## Minion Gear

Minion gear is crucial for boosting minion stats. When evaluating minions, always compare them unequipped for a fair assessment.

*   **Prioritization:** Always prioritize gear that boosts "form %" over non-form specific gear.
*   **Specific Recommendations:**
    *   **Zombie:** Focus on upgrading the Legendary Dethrone Coffin and Mind Control Helmet.
    *   **Angel:** Upgrade Minerva Staff and the Sixth Path.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Apply leftover materials to gear that boosts secondary minion types or special stats, such as Trace of Lilith, Mind Control Helmet, or Sixth Path.
*   **Hulc Gear:** Hulc MK gear is generally considered inefficient and should not be used unless you are specifically running a Demon minion at Tier 8 (T8).

## Rocket Cabins

Rocket Cabins provide significant boosts to minion stats, either for a specific minion type or all minions.

*   **Enhancements:** Relics and resonances can greatly enhance the effects of cabins that boost minion stats. The third row of cabins specifically boosts a particular minion type, and relics/resonances can further boost their hard stats.
*   **Upgrade Strategy:** Focus on upgrading cabins that directly benefit your primary minion type. For example, if you are using Zombies, consider breaking down Space-Sensing Radar, Rainbow Generator, and Gamma Ray to upgrade EMP as high as possible. Use the leftover materials to rebuild the other three cabins.

## Army Composition

Optimal army compositions can differ significantly between game modes like Species War and Minion Sim. Generally, clone your strongest minions unless you have a specific cloning objective.

### Species War Strategy

*   **Optimal Minions:** Demon and Mutant minions are often optimal due to their high ATK and Rush stats.
*   **Casualty Reduction:** Deploying a handful of high HP, low power troops in the first slot can help limit casualties, allowing you to survive all 20 rounds more effectively.

### Minion Sim Strategy

*   **Optimal Minions:** Angels and Zombies are excellent choices for Minion Sim. Their high HP allows them to extend fights to later rounds, helping you clear further, even though you will likely die before round 20.

### Minion Tiers & Effectiveness

*   **Dragons:** Dragons are generally not optimal until they reach Tier 8 (T8). Players have noted that their Mutant T8 minions surpassed Dragons in effectiveness, and Zombie T8 also became more viable than Dragons. Demon T8 was still considered worse than Dragons in one player's experience.
*   **Skins:** Minion skins are crucial. Prioritize orange skins over blue skins whenever possible, as the stat difference between them scales with your overall strength. If your minions lack skins, consider replacing them with another minion type that has them.

## Minion Comparison & Rating

While minion rating serves as a general benchmark, it's important to understand that it doesn't always directly translate to practical strength, and "snail power" isn't a perfect indicator.

*   **Fair Comparison:** To accurately compare minion strengths, always unequip all gear before assessing their base stats and ratings.
*   **Practicality vs. Rating:** Although using your highest-rated minions is generally a good strategy, there are situational exceptions. For instance, lower-rated zombies with high HP and defense can be strategically placed in the first slot in Species War to absorb damage and reduce casualties.
*   **Minion Slotting:** In Species War, the minion with the lowest overall power will be placed in the first slot. This can be exploited to minimize losses. For example, a Sanctum Warrior (5400 HP) in the first slot might result in ~40 minions lost per token, compared to a Frost Drake (3500 HP) in the first slot leading to ~60 minions lost per token. This difference becomes significant over multiple tokens.

## Key Takeaways

*   Prioritize minion gear that boosts "form %" and upgrade specific legendary gear for your main minion types (e.g., Dethrone Coffin for Zombies, Minerva Staff for Angels).
*   Strategically upgrade Rocket Cabins to match your primary minion type, even if it means breaking down other cabins for materials.
*   Army composition varies by game mode: use high ATK/Rush minions (Demon/Mutant) for Species War, and high HP minions (Angels/Zombies) for Minion Sim to extend fights.
*   Dragons are generally not optimal until T8; consider Mutant or Zombie T8 as stronger alternatives.
*   Always compare minions *unequipped* for an accurate assessment, and remember that minion rating isn't the sole indicator of effectiveness; strategic placement and high HP can be more valuable in certain situations.
