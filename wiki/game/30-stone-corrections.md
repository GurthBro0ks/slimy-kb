# 30 Stone Corrections

## Cosmos Revive Count

Cosmos has 3 revives.

## Realm Traits vs. Organs

Realm traits and organs are distinct game systems and should not be described as the same or interchangeable.

## Reverse Scale Source

The Reverse Scale item is obtained from the Apostle Purge Pack. It is believed to come from the second or third paid Apostle Purge Pack, costing approximately $40 to $60. The exact pack number and price are approximate and subject to confirmation by newer verified sources.
