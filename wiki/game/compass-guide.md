# Compass Relic Guide

Guide for optimal compass relic setups across all game areas. Compiled from player-written guides in the SlimyInvertabrates Discord.

## Farm Compass

- Use **Unispark** as the base.
- If you have orange stamps, you can also use **Golden Bird**, **Holy Loaves**, or **Voyager** instead — roughly same value.
- Sort by compass relics and equip your best element damage relics, or stats like crit, def ignore, and crit damage to maximize damage output.

## Push Compass

- Equip **Unispark** as base.
- Sort by compass relics and equip your best element damage relics and stats like crit, def ignore, or crit damage.
- If you care to optimize further, swap in the best element damage relics you have.

## Gene Sim Compass

Two variants depending on whether you have **Rush**:

**With Rush:**
- Use the standard gene sim compass setup with rush-enabled relics.

**Without Rush:**
- Use **Potato** and specific green relics to boost the affect check significantly lower.
- **Crystal Skull** (purple relic, 10% crit) is a good fill-in if you don't have the listed relics.
- Contact an experienced player for further substitute options.

## Arena Compass

- Many recommended relics are P2W, but **Heimdall** and **Ultimate Traveler Manual** should be maxed by most players — especially the manual.
- After the high-value relics, sort by arena stamps and equip the best element damage and utility stats you have.
- Fill any missing slots with arena stamp relics that provide element damage or similar offensive stats.

### Arena Slot Strategy

- Copy the recommended setup exactly.
- For any relics you don't have, fill those slots with arena stamp relics providing element damage.
- The recommended relics represent the best possible — after those, it's incremental boosts.
- Some players use relics like **Boltzmann** or **Liberty Guide** for museum boosting, but these sacrifice arena performance. If you prioritize arena pushing, use dedicated arena relics instead.
- **Gueverra** (top relic) should be equipped if available (obtainable from purple stamps via ANS Synth with orange).

### Civ/Tech/Art Slots

- For missing slots, fill with the best arena relics you have.
- Civ and faith slots should maintain high affect numbers — don't sacrifice too much attack for utility.
- If using both **Nibelungenlied** and **Ultimate Traveler**, put one in tech and the other wherever it fits best.

## Key Takeaways

- Unispark is the go-to base compass for farm and push
- Arena compass prioritizes Heimdall and Ultimate Traveler Manual above all else
- Gene sim without rush requires Potato + specific greens to lower the affect check threshold
- Fill missing P2W slots with arena stamp relics (element damage, crit, etc.)
- Museum-boosting relics (Boltzmann, Liberty Guide) can be used but sacrifice arena performance
- Gueverra top relic is obtainable from purple stamps via ANS Synth
