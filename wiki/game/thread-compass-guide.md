# Thread Compass Guide

## Compass Guide

This guide provides recommendations for Compass relic setups across various game modes. The goal is to optimize performance for farming, pushing, Gene Simulation, and Arena combat.

### Farm Compass

For maximizing farming efficiency, the primary focus is on relics that boost resource generation.

*   **Core Relics:** Holy Loaves, Voyager
*   **Alternative:** Golden Bird (if you have orange stamps for it, offers similar value to the core relics).

### Push Compass

When pushing through content, the focus shifts to maximizing damage output.

*   **Core Relic:** Unispark
*   **General Strategy:** Beyond Unispark, sort your relics by "Compass" and equip those that provide the best elemental damage, critical hit chance, defense ignore, or critical damage bonuses. Prioritize relics that directly contribute to overall damage.

### Gene Simulation Compass

Optimizing your Compass for Gene Simulation can significantly improve your progress, especially if you are struggling with "rush" requirements.

*   **With Rush:** The standard setup focuses on general combat effectiveness. The Crystal Skull (purple relic) is a good fill-in option, providing +10% Crit.
*   **Without Rush (or needing more Rush):** If you lack sufficient rush or need to lower the "affect check" significantly, specific green relics are recommended. The "Potato" relic, along with other specific green relics, can drastically reduce the affect check, allowing for deeper runs.

*(Note: Two images were provided in the original discussion, one for each setup.)*

### Arena Compass

Arena setups often involve powerful, sometimes "Pay-to-Win" (P2W), relics. However, some essential relics are generally accessible and should be prioritized.

*   **Essential Relics:**
    *   Heimdall
    *   Ultimate Traveler Manual (should be maxed by most players)
*   **General Strategy:** After equipping the essential relics, fill any remaining slots with other relics that have "Arena" stamps. Prioritize those that offer elemental damage, critical hit chance, defense ignore, or critical damage.
*   **Relic Prioritization:**
    *   The discussion highlighted the importance of powerful combat relics like Ultimate Traveler Manual, Nibelungenlied, and Gueverra (Che Guevara's Diary). These are considered "best possible relics" that "need to have on" for optimal arena performance.
    *   If you have relics equipped primarily for museum boosts (e.g., Boltzman, Liberty Guide for S+ museums), consider replacing them with combat-focused arena relics if your goal is to maximize arena pushing and win rate. While museum boosts are valuable, they may hinder arena performance compared to dedicated combat relics.
*   **Stamps and Organs:** High-level arena stamps on your relics and an optimized organ setup (refer to the Organ Guide) are crucial for competitive arena play, often making a significant difference even against opponents with higher overall power.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Context Matters:** Compass setups are highly dependent on the game mode (Farm, Push, Gene Sim, Arena).
*   **Prioritize Core Relics:** Certain relics (e.g., Unispark for Push, Heimdall/Ultimate Traveler for Arena) are considered essential for their respective modes.
*   **Adapt for Gaps:** If you lack specific recommended relics, fill slots with the best available alternatives that align with the mode's objective (e.g., element damage for combat, specific greens for Gene Sim rush).
*   **Combat vs. Utility:** For competitive modes like Arena, prioritize combat-boosting relics over those offering passive benefits like museum boosts, especially if you are struggling to win.
*   **Stamps and Organs are Crucial:** Beyond the base relics, high-tier stamps and an optimized organ setup significantly impact performance in combat-focused modes.
