# Thread Organs

## Organ Acquisition Priority

*   **Seasonal Shop (Gold Punch Points):**
    1.  Prioritize acquiring one copy of each of the three missing red organs: Gold Finger, Mecha Arm, and Invisible Wing.
    2.  Once these organs are unlocked, switch to exclusively buying the 2,500 packs with gold points, as this is the only method to obtain additional copies of these specific organs.
*   **Personal Upgrade Path:** One player recommends focusing on upgrading the middle organ helmet to Tier 2, then switching to the top eye organ until it also reaches Tier 2.

## Arena Organ Setups

*   **General Recommendation:**
    *   Experiment with different organs, such as swapping between a top left orange helmet and a red one. The orange helmet, despite slightly less ATK%, may offer better overall stats due to additional Crit DMG%.
    *   Two specific orange organs are considered essential:
        *   One provides 2 revives if you are running Cosmos, or 1 revive if using Mirror.
        *   Another orange organ on the left side provides a solid heal.
*   **Dodge % Effectiveness in Arena:**
    *   A player advises against using the bottom "texture" organ if it provides Dodge %.
    *   Dodge % is generally considered worthless in Arena because the majority of opponents will have 100%+ Hit, effectively reducing your Dodge chance to 0%. It is recommended to swap this organ out for Arena battles.

## Pushing Areas Organ Setups

*   Experimentation is key. An orange organ setup may be more effective than a red one for pushing through areas.

## Dominating Organ Setups

*   **Strategic Organ Removal:**
    *   For Dominations, it is intentionally recommended to remove certain organs from your setup.
    *   The primary goal in Dominations is to secure a win within 3 rounds. Organs that proc damage in later rounds (e.g., dealing less damage in Round 3 and more in Round 4) can be detrimental to achieving this swift victory.
    *   Removing such organs eliminates the chance of them randomly proccing in Round 3, ensuring a more consistent and front-loaded damage output.
    *   This strategy aims for a guaranteed 530% of your ATK as damage in Round 3, broken down as:
        *   210% from pre-round effects.
        *   210% from attacking organs (whichever proc).
        *   110% from an on-hit texture organ.

## Gene Sim Organ Setups

*   If you do not possess the orange abdomen organ obtained from Apostle Purges, use the red abdomen organ as an alternative.

## Key Takeaways

*   **Organ Acquisition:** Prioritize specific red organs (Gold Finger, Mecha Arm, Invisible Wing) from the Seasonal Shop, then use 2,500 packs for additional copies.
*   **Arena Strategy:** Focus on organs that provide Crit DMG%, revives, and heals. Avoid organs that grant Dodge % in Arena, as it is largely ineffective against high enemy Hit stats.
*   **Dominating Strategy:** For quick 3-round victories in Dominations, strategically remove organs that might delay damage or proc in later rounds to ensure consistent early-round burst.
*   **Flexibility:** Be prepared to experiment with different organ combinations for various game modes (Arena, Pushing, Dominating, Gene Sim) and substitute missing orange organs with red alternatives when necessary.
