# Question

What should be researched?

# What matters

- Identify the actual decision this research should support.
- State what evidence would count as a useful harvest.
- Keep owner-only constraints explicit.

# Out of scope

- Anything that belongs in a later forage.

# Constraints

- Keep all resulting artifacts under `research/`.
- Do not expose outputs publicly.

# Acceptance for the harvest

- A clear `report.md`.
- Structured sources and citations.
- A `critic.md` and `RESULT.md` in the proof burrow.
